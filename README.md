# Elyra · The Keepers of the Remnant

Walkable WebGL chapters from the Vista 3D series, built with three.js r128 (cdnjs) as plain static files. Open `index.html` or deploy the folder as a static site; there is no build step.

## Performance pass (Nov 12)

Bryce: "optimize the entire game for performance while preserving its current visual quality". Nothing a level builds was removed or restyled; the work is in what gets drawn, how far away a lighter copy is used, and memory.

- **`js/shared/perf.js`** (last script on every level page, replacing `pc-boost.js`):
  - **Graphics presets**: Performance / Balanced / Quality (`localStorage elyra.preset`; phones default Balanced, PCs Quality; the old "Low" maps to Performance). In a level the Settings button reads "Graphics: …" and cycles them; the main menu's Settings has the same three.
  - **Adaptive resolution** inside each preset's range (Balanced: 72–100% of the level's own resolution), stepping down below 47 fps and back up above 57; a step down that didn't raise the frame rate is undone (GPU not the bottleneck, e.g. iOS Low Power Mode). PC Quality still supersamples up to 1.5x while the frame rate holds.
  - **Culling**: instanced sets and skinned characters had culling switched off (three.js r128 culls them by the wrong bounds), so they were drawn behind the camera and in every shadow pass. They get correct bounds (all instances / the bones as posed this frame). Big instanced sets spread over a map draw, per frame, only the instances in view or in the sun's shadow area. Lit, non-glowing meshes under about half a pixel on screen are skipped. No hardware occlusion queries: r128 has none, and their frame of latency shows as pop-in.
  - **Automatic LOD**: indexed meshes over 2,000 triangles get up to three lighter copies made in a worker (`js/vendor/meshopt_simplifier.js`), sharing the original vertex buffers (only a new index list is stored). The copy drawn is the lightest whose measured error is under the preset's pixel budget (0.6 px Quality, 1 px Balanced, 2 px Performance), so switches are below what the eye can see and big landmarks stay at full detail at any distance. The original geometry stays on the mesh outside the draw (raycasts, collisions untouched).
  - **GPU memory** (phones, and Performance everywhere): geometry/textures over 256 KB that have been out of every scene for 5 minutes are released from the GPU; three.js re-uploads them if they come back.
  - **Stats**: Settings → Performance stats (F8 on PC, or `?perf` in the address): fps, 1% low, draw calls, triangles, GPU memory estimate, render scale, LOD/culling counts, load time, MB downloaded. `ELYRA_PERF.report()` returns it all.
- **KTX2 phone set, `models/k/`** (`tools/ktx2.mjs`, from `models/m/`): colour/emissive/ORM textures as ETC1S, normal maps as UASTC, all mipmapped; the GPU keeps them compressed (iPhone: ETC2/ASTC). Colour maps take about 1/8 of the memory of decoded WebP. 177 files, 62.7 → 77.3 MB on disk. `js/shared/models.js` loads `js/vendor/ktx2-loader.js` (three r128's KTX2Loader bundled as a classic script) on phones and reads `k/` first, falling back file by file to `m/`, then `models/`. `localStorage elyra.ktx2 = off` turns it off. After rebuilding a phone model, redo its KTX2 copy (needs KTX-Software's `toktx` on PATH): `node tools/ktx2.mjs models/m models/k`
- **Pools**: rockets (`fireRocket`) and thrown grenades are reused instead of new meshes and materials per shot (bolts, flashes, smoke, missiles and soldiers were already pooled).

## Phone set (Nov 11)

Phones load every model from `models/m/`, a lighter copy of `models/` (PCs keep `models/` and `models/pc/` exactly as before). Made offline by `tools/mobile.mjs`: colour textures at most 512 px on characters, guns, vehicles, ships and other close-up models and 256 px on props and set pieces; normal/roughness/metal maps 256 px; anything over 30k triangles cut toward it. The Last Signal on a phone went from ~540 MB of textures on the GPU to ~240 MB. The switch is in `MODELS.loader()` (`MOBILE_SET`: phones on Model detail auto or light; a file missing from `m/` falls back to `models/`).

**After adding or replacing a model**, rebuild its phone copy (needs `npm i @gltf-transform/core @gltf-transform/extensions @gltf-transform/functions meshoptimizer sharp` once, anywhere):

```
CLOSE='^(chars/|gun-|fp-arms|pistol|sniper|launcher|herald-rifle|hollow-gun|jetpack|interceptor|raven|vanguard|wraith|harrower|dropship|ship_|hog-|mech|herald-mech|tide-|michael|elias|hollow-king|host-trooper|marine|engineer|civilian|heavy|hollow-heavy|herald-heavy|watcher|turret|weapon-rack|weapon-case|ammo-crate|frag|fighter-cockpit|nav-table|voidclass)'
node tools/mobile.mjs models models/m --color 512 --detail 256 --tris 30000 --skip '^(pc|m|p|incoming)/' --match "$CLOSE"
node tools/mobile.mjs models models/m --color 256 --detail 256 --tris 30000 --skip "^(pc|m|p|incoming)/|$CLOSE"
```
(Only files newer than their phone copy are redone; add `--force` to redo everything.)

**PC set (Nov 11, later):** PCs on Model detail auto read `models/p/` (copies of `models/` and `models/pc/`): every colour texture at full size, only normal/roughness/metal/AO maps halved (1024 px on the close-up list, 512 px on the rest). Pathfinder on PC: ~1.58 GB → ~1.25 GB of GPU textures. Model detail: full reads the originals. Rebuild after adding a model:
```
node tools/mobile.mjs models models/p --color 4096 --detail 1024 --tris 999999999 --skip '^(m|p|incoming)/' --match "^(pc/)?${CLOSE#^}"
node tools/mobile.mjs models models/p --color 4096 --detail 512 --tris 999999999 --skip "^(m|p|incoming)/|^(pc/)?${CLOSE#^}"
```

## TakeOver · Gilded Shore (Oct 30)

`multiplayer.html` (**TakeOver** on the menu, renamed from Multiplayer Oct 30). Team deathmatch, Keepers against Keepers in team colours: you and eleven bots in blue against twelve in red (yellow and green are ready in `GS.TEAMS`). First to 50 kills or the most after 12 minutes. Bots only for now; real players on two devices (peer-to-peer over WebRTC, joined by a room code, nothing to host) is the next pass.

- **The island** (`js/mp/01-world.js`): a square 640 m across (`GS.HALF` = 320; Oct 30 it was cut from 1,500 m after Bryce's first play), a beach and the sea all round. ## Code layout

Classic scripts that share one global scope, loaded in the order each page lists them.

- `js/shared/core.js`: seeded random numbers, noise, geometry merger, helpers.
- `js/shared/models.js`: the model loader and auto-rig (see Models).
- `js/shared/fleets.js`: enemy ships from the design sheet: the Heralds' Harrower, Wraith fighter and Void Lancer; the Hollow King's Harbinger, Voidclass cruiser and Devourer bomber. Global `SHIPKIT`.
- `js/shared/ships.js`: Keeper ships and older shared models.
- `js/shared/damage.js`: the damage system from Bryce's Combat Master spec (`docs/COMBAT-SPEC.md`): weapon damage by hit zone, 100 HP + 25 shield pools with carry-through, shield regeneration, the medical supply, vehicle and ship classes, aircraft weapons, threat-level NPC damage and hit chance. Global `DMG`.
- `js/shared/fx.js`, `js/shared/combat.js`: bolts, explosions, smoke; hulls, ship tags, damage fires, homing missiles.
- `js/shared/shiphud.js`: the HUD strip: Zeal, the shield gem, hull (HP on foot).
- `js/shared/audio.js`: all sound, synthesized with WebAudio, and the radio voices. On iPhone, speech unlocks on the first real tap; lines that never start fall back to reading time so cutscenes keep their pace. Lines marked `alert` are said harder and faster.
- `js/shared/cutscene.js`: camera keyframes, letterbox, titles, subtitles paced by the voices, Skip. Widens the view on a phone held upright.
- `js/shared/progress.js`: points, objectives, challenges, achievements, Remnant Marks, paints, threat levels, the briefing card.
- `js/shared/pause.js`: pause (button, Esc or P). On phones it also holds Sound and Quality, which used to sit in a corner Menu.
- `js/shared/prologue.js`: the prologue before The Mountain, ten painted shots drawn in code and narrated through the shared voices.
- `js/menu/the-call.js`, `footage.js`, `call-sound.js`: the recovered footage (3D), the menu's idle secret, the menu music.
- `js/origin/`: Archive 01. `01-world.js` the two canyons, terrain, sky, the Hollow, the signal; `02-people.js` the code-built people and their poses; `03-play.js` movement, weapons (Pulse rifle, Lance, Breaker), gear (the Q/X gear slots: night vision, wall sight, shield, sentry, grapple, decoy, camo, thrusters, overshield, EMP, echo scanner, medic drone; Zeal), pickups, turrets, troopers, dropships, squad and the lane-aware pathfinding; `04-story.js` the phases, cutscenes and the command deck; `05-audio.js` audio glue; `06-below.js` below the Hollow (the tunnel's walkable shape is `BNODES`/`BCH` in `01-world.js`, built far west of the map at x -2400).
- `js/space/`: Archive 02. `js/tide/`: Archive 03 (`11-story.js` holds the Spear, `15-heralds.js` the Herald convoy).

## Controls

**The Mountain, PC:** WASD move, mouse aims (click to lock), left click fires, right click aims down the sights (the Lance has a scope), 1 2 3 4 or the wheel switch weapons (4: the sidearm), R reloads, Q mobile shield, X shoulder sentry, G or F grenade, Space jump, C or Ctrl crouch, Z Zeal, Shift sprint; walk up to your interceptor (or E) to board.
**The Mountain, phone:** left thumb moves (push to the edge to sprint), right thumb aims, double-tap the right side to aim down the sights; FIRE (swipe up off it for a grenade), NADE, GEAR (tap: the Q gear, hold: the X gear; night vision and wall sight to start, picked in the Armory's Gear tab), JUMP, CROUCH; walk up to your interceptor to board. The small ammo box reloads and the small ⇄ switches weapons. Zeal (every chapter): tap the shield gem when it turns gold.
**Full screen on iPhone:** Safari won't hide its bars for a page, so the game ships a web-app manifest: Share → Add to Home Screen, then play from the icon.
**Flight (The Heralds, The Empty Sky):** mouse aims, right-click guns, left-click or F missiles, WASD fly, Space/Q up and down, Shift boost, Z Zeal. Phone: left thumb flies, right thumb aims, FIRE, MSL, ZEAL. Tide City: G (PC) or GUN (phone) crews the nearest sea gun.
**Everywhere:** pause with the button at the top left, Esc or P; M mutes; Enter skips a cutscene.

Backups of earlier versions live on the `backup-*` branches. What's left to build is in `ROADMAP.md`.
