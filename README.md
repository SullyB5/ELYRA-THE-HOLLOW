# Elyra · The Keepers of the Remnant

Walkable WebGL chapters from the Vista 3D series, built with three.js r128 (cdnjs) as plain static files. Open `index.html` or deploy the folder as a static site; there is no build step.

## TakeOver · Gilded Shore (Oct 30)

`multiplayer.html` (**TakeOver** on the menu, renamed from Multiplayer Oct 30). Team deathmatch, Keepers against Keepers in team colours: you and eleven bots in blue against twelve in red (yellow and green are ready in `GS.TEAMS`). First to 50 kills or the most after 12 minutes. Bots only for now; real players on two devices (peer-to-peer over WebRTC, joined by a room code, nothing to host) is the next pass.

- **The island** (`js/mp/01-world.js`): a square 640 m across (`GS.HALF` = 320; Oct 30 it was cut from 1,500 m after Bryce's first play), a beach and the sea all round. Streets run three ways on a triangular lattice, so blocks are triangles, with two zigzag boulevards through the spire and now and then a whole cell as one long hall. The city is in quarters, each with its own mix (`QMIX`): the **civic** heart (domes in four profiles, colonnades you can walk under, lantern towers, fountains), the **gardens** to the north-west (terraces, roof gardens, pergolas, sloped wedges), the **market** to the south-east (stalls under coloured awnings, open pavilions), the **shore** ring (beach bars with parasols and loungers, wedges, sails), and the **wards** by the bases (stepped blocks, halls, towers, team banners on the walls). Every building also picks its own stone, trim (gold, sea-teal, terracotta, copper, blue), window style (bands, slits, round ports, fins, or the gold lattice on blank stone), glass tint, roof colour and roof (plain, garden, sails, pyramid, tanks), all from a hash of where it stands. Four hills with pines and rock, rock stacks on the two open corners, palms, street lamps, and four grey shore structures out over the water.
- **Levels you can walk (Oct 30, second pass)**: the north-east half is **highlands**, 24 m above the south-west lowlands, rising across the city east of the spire; red's base sits up there. Two **grand halls** (hexagonal rings of three storeys round a courtyard, 100 m across, passages through at street level) and enterable **keeps** (three storeys) have stairs up to their roofs, window openings you can see and shoot through, floors with stairwells and parapets on the roofs. **Bridges** (29) run from those roofs to the roofs around them and over the **alleys** that split some blocks in two. The lowlands hold a **defence line** facing the rise: three watchtowers, four bunkers with firing slits and a stair to the roof, and gold-capped ramparts with blue pennants, plus a picket of two more towers and a bunker out in front of blue's base (Oct 30). On a slope, a keep, tower or bunker stands on a stone plinth level with the high side, with a gold line where the plinth ends and a flight of steps down from each door. The small domes became **ziggurats** with a stair up one face to a shrine. Past the paving, the shore is grass, bushes and long grass down to the sand, with rocky bluffs under the highlands.
- **How storeys work**: `S3` in `js/mp/01-world.js` holds walls (boxes with a height range), floors you stand on and slabs that stop shots, in a 10 m grid; `floorY3(x,z,y)` / `playD3(x,z,y)` give the floor and the walls at a height. `js/origin/03-play.js` uses them when a map defines them (moveBody, stepPlayer, jumping, grenades, keepIn); every other level falls back to `groundY` / `playD` and plays as before. Bots walk every level too (Oct 30, next line).
- **Past the sea** (`js/shared/vista.js`, shared so the other levels can take it next): a snow-capped massif with stepped sea cliffs, waterfalls and a white tower beyond the red corner, two lower ones elsewhere, plateau islands, continents with ranges along the horizon, and a faint skyline ring. Light and haze are baked into vertex colours, so it costs a few draw calls.
- **Bases**: blue south-west, red north-east, 62 m across the radius. A walled ring with three gates and a dome no shot crosses either way; the other side is pushed out of it, on foot, in a hog or in the air. Ten random spawn points, three gun racks with ammo, three warthogs and three craft on pads.
- **Warthogs** (`js/shared/vehicles.js`, `addRover(...,{weap})`): chaingun (as before), rocket (two rockets at once, each the walker cannon's: 250 on a direct hit plus its blast) and laser (300 a hit, no blast). Rocket and laser share one fire rate (1 a second, `HOG_HEAVY_RPS`); the chaingun keeps its own.
- **Craft** (`js/mp/02-air.js`): Herald fighter, Keeper fighter, interceptor; The Heralds' flight scaled to the island. E (USE) at a pad to take off; E low and slow to land and get out, higher up to bail. Guns: right-click (phone FIRE), missiles: left-click or F (MSL). Each side also keeps AI fighters up that dogfight and strafe: four a side on PC, two on a phone (doubled Oct 30). **Missiles lock on the ground too (Oct 30)**: the other side's soldiers and AI hogs; a ground lock lofts over the roofs and drops on its target (a direct hit downs a soldier; two take a hog).
- **AI warthogs (Oct 30)** (`js/mp/04-hogs.js`): each side keeps as many hogs on the streets as it has craft in the air (four on PC, two on a phone), a driver and a gunner in the side's colours, one of each gun in turn. They leave by their base's gates, drive the street graph, circle what they find at gun range, run down soldiers on foot, and come back 20 s after they're lost (a point to whoever downs one). Your guns, rockets and grenades find the other side's; their bots and craft shoot at yours.
- **Bots** (`js/mp/03-match.js`): Keeper soldiers tinted by team, taking the nearest enemy they can see, closing to range and side-stepping. **Navigation (Oct 30)**: `GS.nav` is a graph over the street lattice plus every floor and roof of the halls, keeps, towers and bunkers (a loose grid of nodes on each), the doors, the foot and head of every flight, and both ends of every bridge; a flight or a bridge is one link between levels, and same-level nodes are linked where a walk between them stays on that floor. A bot walks straight at where it wants to go when that's on its level and clear, otherwise node to node (`navStep`), keeps to its level (it won't walk off a roof or into a stair hole), and about one roaming goal in six is a **perch**: a tower top, a roof or a bridge, where it stays 7 to 16 s and holds it, shooting whatever comes in range. Red ones are in `ENEMIES`, so your guns, rockets, grenades and hogs find them; there's no friendly fire.
- Test links: `multiplayer.html?skip=1` skips the opening flyover.

## TakeOver · Rainveil (Oct 30)

`jungle.html` (Multiplayer on the menu opens the map list). A small night jungle in the rain, on foot only: no craft, no hogs, eight a side, first to 50 or the most after 10 minutes (`GS.CFG` in `js/mp/jungle-world.js`; `js/mp/03-match.js` reads it, Gilded Shore's settings are its defaults).

- **The map**: 144 m square, the same turned half way round so neither side has the better ground. Blue's outpost west, red's east, each a stone ring under a faint dome with sandbag walls toward the trees. Three trails across (north, middle, south), a creek down the middle, a stepped temple on a mound in the north-east with a glowing glyph-stone (its twin shrine in the south-west), broken walls and fallen logs along the trails (`S3` walls: they stop walking, shots and sight). About 200 buttressed trees and 90 understory palms (trunks are cover), lianas, and instanced ferns, bushes and broad leaves everywhere off the trails.
- **The storm**: dense fog under the canopy (about 40 m of sight), rain streaks round the camera, a synthesised rain hiss, lightning every 10 to 24 s with thunder after it, fireflies.
- **Flashlights**: yours is on from the start, F toggles it (phone: LIGHT), and it casts shadows on PC. Every soldier carries one, a pale cone along his aim. Bots see you further with yours on (×1.3) and less far with it off (×0.75).
- Test link: `jungle.html?skip=1`.

## TakeOver · Rime (Oct 30)

`rime.html` (Multiplayer on the menu). A glacier at the bottom of Elyra: the sun a hand above the horizon, the pack ice of a frozen sea past the glacier's front, the wind never stopping. Ten a side, one warthog at each outpost (chaingun; `CFG.baseHogs`), no craft and no AI hogs, first to 50 or the most after 12 minutes. All in `js/mp/rime-world.js`.

- **The map**: 220 m square (`GS.HALF` 110), the same turned half way round. Blue's outpost on a rock outcrop in the west, red's in the east, three ways down off each outcrop for the hog, the Keepers' prefab wall round the back.
- **The crevasses** (`RM_CREV`: Catmull-Rom centrelines, a half width, a depth, a ramp at each end). **The Rift** runs north to south through the middle, 6 m wide and 5 m deep, ramps at both ends and two snow bridges over it. **The ice caves**, one by each outpost (blue's south-west, red's north-east): a ramp down, then a tunnel under the ice with blue light through its roof and three holes to drop through, out into the Rift. **The Teeth**: open trenches across the approaches (north-west, south-east), a bridge over each. Walk off a lip and you drop in; inside, the walls hold you, and you get out by the ramps or the station's slab. A hog can't drive into a crevasse, only over the bridges.
- **The station**: a Keeper research station across the Rift in the middle, its middle fallen in. Each half tilts down toward the break (the mesh is sheared, so the walls stay upright), its broken floor slab leans down into the Rift floor. Windows you shoot through, a door on the outer end and one on the south side, a gun rack in each half (`GS.RACKS`, so a sniper and rockets are up for grabs in the middle), and a roof you reach up the snow drifted against its north side.
- **Cover**: seracs, pressure ridges, boulders, and Hollow skimmers (the `wraith` model, violet) half sunk in the ice.
- **Weather**: snow blowing past in streaks, spindrift snaking over the ice, and every 40 to 75 s a **whiteout** (a banner warns you): the fog pulls in, the far ranges go white, the bots' sight drops to under half (`CFG.see`), the wind rises. The wind sound and the reverb change in the caves and the station (`GS.indoorAt`).
- **Look**: snow with wind-carved sastrugi and glints that catch the light as you move, ice that gives back a little blue light in its depth, a sky with the 22-degree halo and two sun dogs, Astraea pale in the north-east, Elyra's alien sky faint overhead, the shared `VISTA` ranges coloured for ice, bergs in the sea.
- Test link: `rime.html?skip=1`.

## TakeOver · Husk (Oct 30)

`husk.html` (Multiplayer on the menu). A Hollow Voidclass cruiser, gutted and drifting over Astraea. Indoors on two decks (the lower at 0, the upper at 7 m), 160 by 52 m, the same turned half way round. Eight a side, on foot only, first to 50 or the most after 10 minutes. All in `js/mp/husk-world.js`.

- **The plan is a 1 m grid** (`L0`, `L1`, `TOP0`, `TOP1`, `TALL`): each cell has the lower deck's air (0 up to its ceiling), the upper deck's (7 up to its ceiling), or one tall room's all the way up. Every wall, doorway, header, railing, deck and ceiling is worked out from it (`H_WALLS`: where one side of an edge has air and the other doesn't; `H_RAILS`: a deck looking out over a tall room), for walking and shots (S3) and for the meshes, and the bots' graph walks both decks (ramps are their way between them). To change the ship, change the rectangles in `H_ROOMS`, `H_CORR`, `H_DECKS`, `H_RAMPS`.
- **The bays** (blue west, red east): tall rooms with a viewport onto space, a ramp up to a balcony, the dome over each. **The spine**: the upper deck's long corridor (blue's on the north side, red's on the south) with ports onto Astraea. **The reactors**: tall round rooms with the ship's core still glowing, a ramp up to a ring round it, a bridge from the ring to the hangar's gantry. **The holds**: the lower deck's cargo hold (blue's south, red's north), rows of Hollow pods. The main corridor between bay and reactor has a half-shut blast door.
- **The hangar**: 44 by 48 m and 24 high in the middle, its roof torn open to the nebula. **Its gravity plating is gone** (`gravAt` returns 0.3 there): you jump four times as high (about 5 m) and fall slowly, so containers (2.6 m) and the lift platform (3.2 m) are floors; the two-high stacks are cover. Galleries along both long walls, a gantry and catwalks across at the upper deck's height, a ramp up to each gallery, two of the ship's own fighters wrecked on the floor, loose plates drifting.
- **Look**: Bryce's Metal015 and Metal039 on the walls and floors, bone-like ribs down the corridors, violet light lines at every wall's foot, amber in the holds, the bays in their side's colour; the star's light comes in only through the breach and the windows (the hull throws its shadows); outside, the gold nebula, Astraea, three Voidclass cruisers holding off, wreckage tumbling past. A low hum and the hull groaning now and then.
- Test link: `husk.html?skip=1`.

**Engine hooks added for these two (Oct 30)**: a map can set `gravAt(x,y,z)` (js/origin/03-play.js: your jump and fall; js/mp/03-match.js: the bots' fall), `CFG.baseHogs` / `CFG.aiHogs`, `GS.RACKS` (racks out on the map), `GS.HALFZ` (a narrow map's half width for the bots' picks), and `GS.indoorAt` (the reverb). The domes now flash only where a shot hits them (the aim check behind the crosshair used to light the whole dome while you stood in it looking out).

## TakeOver · the kit and four more maps (Oct 31)

All four are small, on foot only, eight a side, first to 50 or the most after 10 minutes. Each is one page and one world file on top of **`js/mp/kit.js`**, the shared kit:

- **The frame**: renderer, scene, camera, bloom and the quality switch, the corner menu.
- **The contract**: `terrH` / `floorY3` / `playD3` / `losBlock` and the rest, built from what the map puts into `S3`: solid boxes in any direction (`KIT.block`), tilted or flat plates you stand on (`KIT.deck`), stairs and planks with a solid underside where it's too low to walk under and rails (`KIT.ramp`), thin walls and railings (`KIT.wall`, `KIT.rail`), round obstacles (`KIT.post`). The map gives its ground (`CITY.ground`) and edge (`CITY.edge`). A wall whose top is within 0.6 m over your feet is a step you take (a stair's last tread onto a roof).
- **The bots' map**: `KIT.nav()` puts a spot every 2 m or so on every floor anyone can stand on (ground, roofs, decks, holds), more along every ramp, joins those you can walk between, and drops anything that doesn't join up with the bases. Perches are high spots away from the domes.
- **Weather and sound**: rain round the camera, a little synth for noise beds and one-shots; a painted reflection cube and neon signs on canvases.

### Midnight Market (`market.html`, `js/mp/market-world.js`)
A block of Tide City at night in the rain, the same turned half way round. **The roofs**: fourteen buildings a side of the street, 5 to 14 m, parapets, water tanks, vents, stair huts; gaps of 2 to 4 m with planks and metal stairs across some, the rest you jump. **The market**: the covered street down the middle with stalls, tarps, lanterns and gun racks; fire escapes and stairs back up. **The towers**: the two 14 m towers either side of the street, joined by a skybridge with the holo-billboard hung under it. **The club** (Bryce's reference: the New Alexandria nightclub): building C's ground floor on each side, EMBER, a dark room with columns of light hanging from the ceiling, a burning sphere over a lit platform, the bar, booths; doors on the street, the alley and the back alley. Blue spawns on its own low roof at the west end, red at the east: a broad stair down, a plank next door, a steep stair up behind. A Keeper patrol craft goes over now and then with its searchlight.

### Vigil Harbour (`harbour.html`, `js/mp/harbour-world.js`)
Tide City's waterfront at night. Quays round a waist-deep basin (wading slows you), warehouses with walkable roofs, container stacks, sodium lamps; a freighter moored down the middle with gangways from both quays, two open holds you drop into, the bridge house with a stair up to its roof; a crane on each long quay with a stair up to its deck; piers and moored boats at the water's edge, steps up out of the water. The lighthouse beam out past the harbour mouth comes round every twelve seconds.

### Beached Freighter (`freighter.html`, `js/mp/freighter-world.js`)
A Keeper freighter (KEEPER TIDEWARD) run aground on an Elyra beach in the late afternoon, rolled 20° toward the sea. The deck slopes from 10.7 m on the dune side down to 4.6 m on the beach side; three holds tilted the same way inside (drop in through a hatch, or walk in through the tear in the middle hold's side), containers slid against the low rail and spilled across the sand (two open at both ends to run through). Stern doors down onto the sand at both ends, stairs up the high side, ladders up the low side. **Mirrored across x = 0**, not turned round (the roll goes one way). The hull's collision is the rolled cross-section in 0.6 m horizontal bands; the deck and hold floors are true tilted plates.

### Fallen Spire (`spire.html`, `js/mp/spire-world.js`)
A tower of the city on its side, broken in two, New Alexandria-style inside (white plaster, warm wood, brushed metal, glass, planters, the old ceiling lights still lit) and the city burning outside (smoke lit orange from below, dark towers with fires in them, smoke columns, embers, Hollow cruisers firing down now and then). Inside, the old storeys are walls every 4.6 m with doorways, stairwells and one broken through; the facade is the floor, its windows pits into the crushed car park 3.5 m below (rubble slopes up in two pits a half and out into the plaza at the break); the far facade is the top, a walk 9 m up with skylights back down into the hall, reached by rubble ramps from the plaza. Side windows are ways out onto the plaza.

## Recovered footage (The Call)

Seven short 3D scenes: Michael's three (01 First Signal, 02 Homebound, 03 Hollow King) and four from the Pathfinder (04 No Contact, 05 Light in the Dark, 06 The White City, 07 Taken, each unlocked by reaching that point in Pathfinder). They play from the **Recovered logs** panel on the menu and the service record drawer, and the idle menu plays the next one this device hasn't seen.

1. **First Signal** (with Archive 01): Michael alone on the command deck at Tide City Command, the red emergency light, the dome dark on the holo table, his terminal facing the bay. He asks ELYRA, the Vigil's ship intelligence, where the Hollow is. *"Because you are already there."* The lights die, the dome wakes, and for a fraction of a second you see inside the Hollow.
2. **Homebound** (with Archive 02): the same room, the dome already awake. The coordinates haven't changed in twenty-three years; Michael hasn't left the facility; the mast camera outside shows Tide City Command abandoned "since the incident", and for one frame something tall standing in the dark. Ends on ACCESS RECORD: MICHAEL — 17 ENTRIES.
3. **Hollow King** (after Archive 02): the night before Tide City, Michael goes back down to the chamber from Archive 01, alone. His name is on the wall, dated the day of Tide City. The Drowned Watcher rises; ELYRA: it has been trying to warn him. About the Spear.
4–6. **No Contact**, **Light in the Dark**, **The White City**: the Pathfinder's own recordings (Okafor's helmet going down, Dropship Two's cockpit, Rhen's eleven seconds whole).
7. **Taken** (after Pathfinder, unlocked by **The White City**): under the King's mountain, the night after the White City. The Hollow King has Rhen's team knelt at the glass of the drowned chamber and calls the Drowned Watcher up to take them; it looks at them and turns away. In the caves their names burn into the wall beside everyone else's, dated 08.03.2237. Rhen calls Tide City; the cave gives his own voice back, slowed: *"Michael."* Every reel's lines, with IDs: `docs/FOOTAGE-SCRIPT.md`.

**The menu's other secrets** (`js/menu/secrets.js`, the 3D in `js/menu/secrets-3d.js`): ping the two small rings in the reticle (sonar: the Drowned Watcher in the dark, closer each ping, then watching you from behind the card), touch the empty dark (a real ship goes over the stars), tap the heartbeat (a second one joins it), tap the signal (a voice comes through with its voice print), or type MICHAEL or RHEN.

The secret still works: leave the menu alone for 5 to 10 minutes and the music bends, the view creeps, the background flickers, the screen goes black and the next footage this device hasn't seen plays (never ahead of the story). After it the menu keeps a blinking red light (1), an access record in the footer (2), and after the third, silence and one more line from ELYRA.

Code: `js/menu/footage.js` builds the three sets in one three.js scene (the deck at the origin, the facility from outside at x +400, the Hollow at x -400) with Michael as the Keeper Marine model posed on the code rig, and runs the scripts; `js/menu/call-sound.js` is the synthesized sound and the voices; `js/menu/the-call.js` is the idle secret, the chips and the after-effects. The 3D (three.js, `core.js`, `02-people.js`, `models.js`) loads only when footage is about to play. Links: `index.html?watch=1` (to `7`; add `&unlock=1` to open sealed ones on this device) shows a tap-to-play card; `?call=1` runs the idle version after 4 seconds.

**The First Word (Oct 26, Level 06)** is The Heralds and The Empty Sky merged into one level, played as three chained parts with no stats or menu between: `tide-city.html?part=streets` (Warden Six in the streets) → `space.html` (The Heralds over Astraea) → `tide-city.html?part=sky` (the Vigil comes home; Tide City's own Herald fleet fight is cut, since The Heralds already has one; the evacuation, the Hollow King, and a FIRE / HOLD choice on the Spear). Tide City's sky uses The Heralds' damage scale. `tide-city.html` with no part still runs streets and sky together for testing.

## Missions on the menu

## Story and factions

The Keepers (white, blue and gold) fight on two fronts. The **Hollow King** and his **Empty Host** (purple, black and silver) are the enemy at home. The **Heralds** of Astraea (red) are the second front; the Pathfinder found them, and its last words were that the Keepers were wrong about them. Lt. Michael Hall of the Interceptor Wing is the player; Master Elias Veyr (his mentor) commands. The canon lore is Bryce's Oct 22 update in `docs/lore-2026-10-22/`; start with `docs/CANON-NOTES.md`, which summarises it and lists what changed in the game.

## Threat level

**Survivor** (the story comes first), **Keeper** (the intended experience; the default) and **Hollow** (nothing is forgiving). Picked on the menu or a chapter's briefing; changing it reloads the chapter. The numbers live in `DIFFS` in `js/shared/progress.js` (ids `easy`, `hard`, `insane`, so old saves carry over). The Mountain's include ammunition, drop rates and health regeneration.

## Code layout

Classic scripts that share one global scope, loaded in the order each page lists them.

- `js/shared/core.js`: seeded random numbers, noise, geometry merger, helpers.
- `js/shared/models.js`: the model loader and auto-rig (see Models).
- `js/shared/fleets.js`: enemy ships from the design sheet: the Heralds' Harrower, Wraith fighter and Void Lancer; the Hollow King's Harbinger, Voidclass cruiser and Devourer bomber. Global `SHIPKIT`.
- `js/shared/ships.js`: Keeper ships and older shared models.
- `js/shared/damage.js`: the damage system from Bryce's Combat Master spec (`docs/COMBAT-SPEC.md`): weapon damage by hit zone, 100 HP + 25 shield pools with carry-through, shield regeneration, the medical supply, vehicle and ship classes, aircraft weapons, threat-level NPC damage and hit chance. Global `DMG`.
- `js/shared/fx.js`, `js/shared/combat.js`: bolts, explosions, smoke; hulls, ship tags, damage fires, homing missiles.
- `js/shared/shiphud.js`: the HUD strip: Zeal, the shield gem, hull (HP on foot).
- `js/shared/audio.js`: all sound, synthesized with WebAudio, and the radio voices. On iPhone, speech unlocks on the first real tap; lines that never start fall back to reading time so cutscenes keep their pace. Lines marked `alert` are said harder and faster.
- `js/shared/cutscene.js`: camera keyframes, letterbox, titles, subtitles paced by the voices, Skip. Widens the view on a phone held upright.
- `js/shared/progress.js`: points, objectives, challenges, achievements, Remnant Marks, paints, threat levels, the briefing card.
- `js/shared/pause.js`: pause (button, Esc or P). On phones it also holds Sound and Quality, which used to sit in a corner Menu.
- `js/shared/prologue.js`: the prologue before The Mountain, ten painted shots drawn in code and narrated through the shared voices.
- `js/menu/the-call.js`, `footage.js`, `call-sound.js`: the recovered footage (3D), the menu's idle secret, the menu music.
- `js/origin/`: Archive 01. `01-world.js` the two canyons, terrain, sky, the Hollow, the signal; `02-people.js` the code-built people and their poses; `03-play.js` movement, weapons (Pulse rifle, Lance, Breaker), gear (mobile shield, shoulder sentry, Zeal), pickups, turrets, troopers, dropships, squad and the lane-aware pathfinding; `04-story.js` the phases, cutscenes and the command deck; `05-audio.js` audio glue; `06-below.js` below the Hollow (the tunnel's walkable shape is `BNODES`/`BCH` in `01-world.js`, built far west of the map at x -2400).
- `js/space/`: Archive 02. `js/tide/`: Archive 03 (`11-story.js` holds the Spear, `15-heralds.js` the Herald convoy).

## Controls

**The Mountain, PC:** WASD move, mouse aims (click to lock), left click fires, right click aims down the sights (the Lance has a scope), 1 2 3 4 or the wheel switch weapons (4: the sidearm), R reloads, Q mobile shield, X shoulder sentry, G or F grenade, Space jump, C or Ctrl crouch, Z Zeal, Shift sprint; walk up to your interceptor (or E) to board.
**The Mountain, phone:** left thumb moves (push to the edge to sprint), right thumb aims, double-tap the right side to aim down the sights; FIRE (swipe up off it for a grenade), NADE, GEAR (tap: shield, hold: sentry), JUMP, CROUCH; walk up to your interceptor to board. The small ammo box reloads and the small ⇄ switches weapons. Zeal (every chapter): tap the shield gem when it turns gold.
**Full screen on iPhone:** Safari won't hide its bars for a page, so the game ships a web-app manifest: Share → Add to Home Screen, then play from the icon.
**Flight (The Heralds, The Empty Sky):** mouse aims, right-click guns, left-click or F missiles, WASD fly, Space/Q up and down, Shift boost, Z Zeal. Phone: left thumb flies, right thumb aims, FIRE, MSL, ZEAL. Tide City: G (PC) or GUN (phone) crews the nearest sea gun.
**Everywhere:** pause with the button at the top left, Esc or P; M mutes; Enter skips a cutscene.

Backups of earlier versions live on the `backup-*` branches. What's left to build is in `ROADMAP.md`.
