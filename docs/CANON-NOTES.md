# Elyra · Canon notes (locked Oct 20, 2026)

The three files next to this one are the canon, as Bryce wrote them. Don't change the lore from here: new work only reveals it, or leaves clues for later missions and games.

- `LORE.md`: the master lore and story bible. Everything that is true in the universe.
- `CAMPAIGN.md`: the 15 missions of the first game. *When* the player learns each piece.
- `PRODUCTION.md`: the production roadmap and recommendations (Bryce's research list; `ROADMAP.md` at the root tracks the build).

## Bryce's rulings on top of the bible (Oct 20)

These settle the places where the bible and the game already built disagreed. Where they conflict with `LORE.md`, these win.

1. **The lore is what's true; the campaign is when we learn it.** Nothing may be revealed earlier than `CAMPAIGN.md` allows, but clues can point at anything.
2. **Earth came first.** Earth was destroyed (the Great Collapse); the arks fled to Elyra. Scripture remembers Heralds appearing at the end like angels with one warning, "something is coming". Most people treat that as a parable. The Spear of Destiny came out of the Collapse with the arks. (The bible's "First Contact War" is how the oldest records name the Collapse.)
3. **The Heralds are met in Archive 02.** Before that they are only heard about (scripture, the Pathfinder's message, the Hollow King). In Archive 02 they turn out to be real, and they are running from something. *Why* they run is not answered yet (probably an energy source or a power nobody can control).
4. **Archive 02 sits late in the full campaign** (Bryce: technically the second-to-last mission). If the full campaign needs the Heralds met earlier, add a **side mission as the Pathfinder**: Captain Rhen's crew arriving at Astraea first. The sealed Pathfinder flight recorder (Archive 02's ending) is the hook for it.
5. **Michael has never been to Astraea, but he has dreamed it.** His sleep log (service record) and the recovered footage are dreams/logs, not things he physically did. They can seed new missions.
6. **The story so far:** Earth destroyed → Elyra → the Hollow King steals the Codex and the Keepers chase him → the trail leads somewhere else (Astraea) → the Heralds are real → they are running too.
7. **The Drowned Watcher** is the sea monster (the "leviathan" in code). Under the Hollow King's mountain is a giant fresh/salt-water lake that runs through the planet and connects to the ocean, so the creature can reach almost anywhere. It is native to Elyra; the King influences it imperfectly; it is not the Hollow.
8. **The Codex** is the Keepers' filing word. Its true name is the **Testament of the First Word**. Suggested link (Claude's, not locked): what the Hollow King stole is the part the Keepers held, and the rest of the archive lies beneath Astraea (Mission 11).
9. **Michael's surname is Hall.** Master Elias Veyr is his mentor, not his father.
10. *(Claude's call, not locked)* **"The Hollow" in Archive 01** is what the Keepers call the Hollow King's mountain, after him. The real Hollow (the cosmic threat) is never entered in the first game; the footage's split-second glimpse stays a dream.

## Bryce's rulings (Oct 21, after the PC playtest)

11. **ELYRA never sent them.** The ship intelligence has no record of taking Michael's team to the Hollow because she didn't. Someone, or something (the Drowned Watcher), did. *(The Call: "No vessel I command has been there.")*
12. **The Drowned Watcher has been warning Michael not to use the Spear,** all along. It is not a Hollow creature. In the third reel it tries to warn him, and ELYRA says so. The reel now comes **after Archive 02, before Tide City**, and Michael's name on the wall is dated 16.03.2237, the day of Tide City, the day the Spear fires.
13. **The Return** (second reel): ELYRA's warning is now *"Do not use the Spear"*. The empty facility "since the incident" is the aftermath of a **Hollow attack**, and the tall thing in the dark is a **Hollow monster**.
14. **Secret ending (to build later):** in Tide City, if the player stops the Spear's charge and keeps both the Hollow King's flagship and the main Herald ship alive, the truth is realized. A hidden ending, not the main one.
15. The Codex isn't just lost in Archive 01: the Watcher takes it and carries it down into the lake that reaches the sea.

## The Pathfinder side mission (Oct 21)

16. **Slipscape** is the name for faster-than-light travel (the old code says "hyperspace" in places; Slipscape is the word from now on).
17. **Side mission S1, Pathfinder, takes place on 08.03.2237.** That is the date carved beside the crew's names under the mountain. Rhen's strike team (Rhen, Marsh, Okafor, Thorne, with Landry flying the dropship) raids an Empty Host installation sunk into a dead moon. They take an object the Host recovered days earlier.
18. **The object is not named or explained.** It isn't Host work and it isn't human. In the crash it shows Landry a shape buried under Astraea. *(Not locked: whether it's tied to the Testament, the containment system or the Codex is for Bryce to decide.)*
19. **The Pathfinder is broken in two in orbit** by Host warships, in front of the dropship. Her halves stay up there, and that is the wreck the Keepers find in Archive 02. The team and the object go down to Astraea in the dropship. It can't make Slipscape without her.
20. The mission ends at "They followed us." Still open for the rest of the arc:
    - surviving on Astraea;
    - repairing the dropship;
    - understanding the object;
    - the Host coming for it;
    - how the crew's names end up carved under the Hollow King's mountain;
    - why Rhen's voice calls Michael from down there.


## The Pathfinder arc and the Hollow (Oct 21, evening)

21. **The Hollow is a force, not a creature.** A mystical evil, like the dark side: it turns people bad and pulls them away from caring for others. It never becomes a physical thing. The Herald civil war is the Hollow at work on them; the cause is never named in the Pathfinder arc.
22. **Pathfinder S2** (draft): the team lands in a Herald civil war in a dead red desert on Astraea, scavenges, and finds the Pathfinder's second dropship.
23. **Pathfinder S3** (draft): a white structure older than the Heralds (the white city of Michael's dream). The team sees what the Heralds ran from (kept off screen), records Rhen's message, and is **taken hostage by the Hollow King's army (the Empty Host)**.
24. **Under the mountain:** the King tries to feed the crew to the Drowned Watcher. It refuses them (it has its own mind), so he keeps them in a prison in the caves. That's why their names are carved there and why Rhen's voice calls Michael. Why he tried the Watcher is open.
25. Draft treatment and continuity check: Claude Docs "Pathfinder · Missions 2 & 3 (draft treatment)".
26. **The timeline (Oct 21, kept simple):** 05.03.2237 the Hollow King takes the Codex and the Pathfinder leaves after him · 08.03 Pathfinder S1 · 09.03 S2 · 10.03 S3, Rhen's message sent, the team taken · 11.03 Archive 01 · 13.03 Archive 01 ending · 14.03 Archive 02 · 16.03 Archive 03. The game's lines now say "six nights ago" (theft), "five days later, she sent us one message", and "a day of silence since the Pathfinder's last message". The names under the mountain carry the date of the theft from the moon, 08.03.
