## CONTINUITY STATUS AND PRECEDENCE

This October 22, 2026 continuity update records decisions approved in discussion after Version 3.0. Read this update before the preserved Version 3.0 text below. Where the original text conflicts, this update takes precedence. Unresolved items are placeholders, not finalized lore. The original material remains intact for development reference; it has not been silently rewritten.

## MAIN CAMPAIGN — STRUCTURAL DIRECTION

Keep the long-term target of 15 main missions, but treat the older mission-by-mission list as a flexible planning draft, not an authoritative schedule. Existing playable prologue and Archives 01–03 anchor the endgame. The other main missions should introduce smaller Archives, logs, dreams, character moments, dogfights, and gradually stronger revelations. Pathfinder S1–S3 is a separate side arc and does not automatically consume three of the 15 main-mission slots.

## ENDING AND SCENE CONFLICTS

In Tide City, the destructive Spear route kills all in its atmospheric discharge and constitutes a moral failure. The secret route should make suppression and resistance meaningful, not claim to kill the Hollow. The King is defeated, not confirmed dead. Existing gameplay conditions for the secret win must be audited before rewriting the final mission. Dialogue or camera orientation about leaving atmosphere while ships face the planet should be corrected subtly, without drawing attention to the inconsistency. Elias’s restricted-record scenes must not make him secretly informed.

## APPROVED UNIVERSAL RULES

The Hollow is a real, necessary, nonphysical universal balancing force. It cannot be killed or eliminated; its destructive influence can be resisted, managed, and greatly suppressed. It is not the Drowned Watcher or a conventional physical boss. The Hollow King recognizes the danger of the Hollow but is fully consumed by its influence. He understands suppression technology and rejects its implications. His fate in the existing game is defeat, not a confirmed death.

## SUPPRESSION TECHNOLOGY

The First Civilization invented the Hollow suppressor; the Heralds recovered and adapted it. Pathfinder steals a suppressor from the Empty Host moon installation on March 8, 2237. The device can project a protective dome of variable size, up to roughly a city (not a country). It requires no external power supply and massively reduces Hollow influence rather than granting immunity or destroying the Hollow. Working explanation, not yet a precisely defined mechanism: its protective strength may be sustained by the people inside actively resisting corruption, and may falter when that resistance collapses.

## HERALDS AND TIDE CITY

The Heralds were originally peaceful and possessed valuable knowledge, including the ancient suppression technology. Hollow corruption and a civil war fractured them. Their attack on Tide City is explained by the combination of civil war, Hollow influence, and misunderstanding; this is the agreed narrative direction, with detailed causes and participants still to be dramatized. Add more distinct Herald ships in the final battle, including ships that can function as combat distractions without implying every Herald is hostile.

## CHARACTERS AND REVELATIONS

Elias Veyr is a genuinely good, ordinary senior Keeper and mentor. He does not secretly possess the truth about Earth or the Hollow, does not intentionally conceal it from Michael, and has no supernatural powers. The Hollow King alone knows the deeper truth about Earth and the Hollow; surviving public histories or records do not automatically convey that full truth. Michael has foreshadowing dreams caused by a combination of Hollow influence and the Drowned Watcher’s mind-to-mind communication, plausibly aided by Elyra’s extensive water. Dreams should feature the Watcher and significant figures, not imply Michael visited Astraea, and should lead toward revelations rather than deliver explanations. Some dreams can become cinematic cutscenes.

## ENDING RULES

In the existing destructive Tide City route, the Spear kills everyone in the atmosphere/area affected by its discharge; Tide City is physically saved but humanity suffers a profound moral failure. Do not silently weaken this rule. The secret true ending is not literal killing of the Hollow: it is resisting/suppressing its corruption, avoiding slaughter of the Heralds, and making coexistence and possible peace achievable. Exact gameplay triggers and the reach of the Spear require alignment with the playable ending; do not replace them with invented conditions.

## PRESERVED MYSTERIES

Michael’s name appears carved beneath the Hollow King’s mountain with the date March 16, 2237. This is intentional and remains unexplained. The Pathfinder crew’s inscription bears March 8, 2237. The Drowned Watcher’s independence, the three recovered-footage reels, the ELYRA AI’s statement that she did not transport Michael’s team to the mountain, the menu secret “The Call,” and the phrase “Second Collapse” remain mysteries or unresolved continuity elements, not licenses to invent explanations.

## PATHFINDER-TO-KEEPERS TRANSFER

Pathfinder S3 ends March 10 with the Empty Host capturing the surviving crew and retaking the suppressor. Between March 11 and March 15, a survivor escapes or is freed from the Hollow King’s mountain, obtains the suppressor, and delivers it to the Keepers. This is an agreed continuity bridge; the survivor’s identity, means of escape, device recovery, and precise delivery scene/date remain open pending Pathfinder S3 and Archive cutscenes.

## PRODUCTION / CAMPAIGN RULE

Existing playable content has priority over the older speculative 15-mission outline: the prologue and Archives 01–03, Pathfinder S1, and existing footage/endings are not to be silently replaced. The goal remains a flexible 15-main-mission campaign, separate from Pathfinder’s three-mission side arc; earlier missions should have their own smaller Archives and escalating revelations. Next priority is Pathfinder S2. No new mission numbering or additional playable levels are approved by this update.

# **ELYRA: THE HOLLOW**

# **DOCUMENT 3 — COMPLETE CAMPAIGN & MISSION DESIGN**

**Version:** 3.0 — Reconstructed Campaign Plan  
**Format:** Linear military science-fiction campaign  
**Long-term target:** 15 main missions, five acts  
**Existing playable content:** Prologue, Archives 01–03, three recovered-footage reels, Pathfinder S1  
**New missions:** Pathfinder S2 and S3

## **1. CAMPAIGN DESIGN PHILOSOPHY**

The campaign begins as a military conflict and gradually becomes a mystery concerning humanity's origins, the Great Collapse, the Heralds, and the universal force known as the Hollow.

The player should initially believe they understand the enemy.

Each major mission must challenge that understanding.

The campaign must combine:

Space combat and Interceptor operations.

Ground combat and military assaults.

Squad-based investigation.

Ancient technology and environmental puzzles.

Survival horror.

Religious and historical mysteries.

Character conflict and difficult decisions.

The Hollow must never become a conventional final boss.

The story's central conflict is whether humanity can resist its destructive influence without destroying the universal balance.

## **2. CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE**

The original campaign contains 15 missions across five acts.

The existing Archives were developed separately and do not map perfectly onto that older structure.

The recommended approach is to preserve the original 15 mission concepts as the full-campaign development target while treating the existing Archives as established story sequences.

Pathfinder S1–S3 form a separate three-mission side arc.

The Archives and Pathfinder missions must retain their established March 2237 chronology.

### **The Original Five Acts**

**Act I — The Known War:** Introduces Michael, the Keepers, the Interceptors, and the first anomalies.

**Act II — Something Is Wrong:** Investigates missing personnel, ancient machinery, and contradictions in Keeper history.

**Act III — The Return:** Escalates the war and reveals the Heralds' true origins.

**Act IV — The Truth Beneath Astraea:** Explores the Hollow's influence and confronts the Hollow King's ideology.

**Act V — The Last Keeper:** Forces Michael to choose between destruction, obedience, and another method of resistance.

The following 15 missions are revised concepts based on the older campaign. Their exact chronological placement relative to the existing Archives remains subject to final restructuring.

# **ACT I — THE KNOWN WAR**

## **MISSION 01 — FIRST WATCH**

**Gameplay:** Space patrol, dogfight, reconnaissance, investigation.

**Location:** Elyran orbital space.

**Premise:** Michael Hall conducts an Interceptor patrol when a missing transport and unexplained signals lead his squad toward an apparent Empty Host attack.

**Objectives:**

1.  Complete an introductory flight patrol.

2.  Investigate the transport's last known location.

3.  Defend against hostile fighters.

4.  Examine wreckage and recover its flight recorder.

5.  Return to Keeper command.

**Story:** The recovered recorder contains a signal unlike ordinary military transmissions. Sensors briefly detect two enormous lights beneath Elyra's ocean.

**Updated lore connection:** The lights foreshadow the Drowned Watcher, not a physical manifestation of the Hollow.

**Ending:** Command classifies the incident as an enemy attack, while the unexplained signal remains active.

**Production note:** This older mission concept may need to become a flashback or earlier patrol because the current Prologue already begins with the Codex theft.

## **MISSION 02 — THE QUIET SETTLEMENT**

**Gameplay:** Ground exploration, scanning, squad combat, extraction.

**Location:** Remote Elyran settlement.

**Premise:** A settlement has stopped responding to Keeper communications.

**Objectives:**

Land outside the settlement.

Search residential buildings.

Restore communications.

Investigate the medical and security facilities.

Locate an unusual signal beneath the settlement.

Defend the extraction area.

**Story:** The settlement appears abandoned without signs of a conventional battle.

Equipment remains active. Personal belongings have been left behind.

A disconnected radio repeats a soldier's voice.

**Updated lore connection:** The event could represent early Hollow influence, an ancient-system malfunction, or deliberate interference. The game should not immediately identify its cause.

**Ending:** The squad discovers a sealed underground access point but withdraws before opening it.

## **MISSION 03 — ORDERS FROM VEYR**

**Gameplay:** Carrier hub, escort, dogfight, pursuit.

**Location:** Human fleet near Elyra.

**Premise:** Master Elias Veyr takes control of the investigation and orders recovered equipment transferred to Keeper custody.

**Objectives:**

Attend Elias's briefing.

Escort the transport.

Defend it against an ambush.

Pursue an escaping hostile craft.

Return to the carrier.

**Story:** The attackers focus on the recovered equipment.

Elias recognizes an ancient symbol but refuses to explain its significance.

**Updated lore connection:** Elias may recognize the symbol from restricted Great Collapse records.

**Ending:** Michael begins questioning whether his mentor is withholding information.

# **ACT II — SOMETHING IS WRONG**

## **MISSION 04 — THE BURIED SIGNAL**

**Gameplay:** Ground combat, ancient exploration, machinery puzzles.

**Location:** Underground complex beneath the settlement.

**Premise:** The signal is traced to a structure older than humanity's settlement of Elyra.

**Objectives:**

Enter the underground complex.

Restore power.

Protect the technical team.

Recover an ancient component.

Investigate an impossible corridor.

Escape the structure's activation.

**Story:** Michael's Zeal interacts with the machinery.

An inscription reads:

"They came before the end."

**Updated lore connection:** The structure may belong to the First Civilization.

**Ending:** Elias orders the site sealed.

## **MISSION 05 — THE EMPTY CHAPEL**

**Gameplay:** Survival horror, limited combat, investigation.

**Location:** Abandoned Remnant religious site.

**Premise:** A Keeper research expedition disappears after investigating a chapel connected to forbidden historical records.

**Objectives:**

1.  Search the chapel.

2.  Restore emergency power.

3.  Find the missing expedition's equipment.

4.  Recover its recordings.

5.  Escape an unidentified physical threat.

**Story:** The chapel contains symbols resembling Herald markings.

A recording states:

"The stories were never about angels coming to save us."

**Updated lore connection:** The threat can be a sentient or corrupted organism. It is not the Hollow itself.

**Ending:** Michael escapes with evidence that Remnant scripture may preserve distorted accounts of real historical events.

## **MISSION 06 — THE FORBIDDEN RECORD**

**Gameplay:** Stealth, restricted archive investigation, security systems.

**Location:** Keeper Archive.

**Premise:** Michael investigates records that contradict the official account of the Great Collapse.

**Objectives:**

Access a restricted archive section.

Recover original records.

Compare official and classified accounts.

Avoid or disable security.

Confront Elias about the findings.

**Story:** Michael discovers evidence that the Heralds were refugees and that humanity's use of the Spear had unintended consequences.

**Updated lore connection:** Since Earth was lost only 83 years earlier, some records may include eyewitness testimony from people still alive.

**Ending:** Michael realizes that the Keepers have not told humanity the complete truth.

**Continuity note:** This mission cannot reveal the Heralds' true history before the established Archive 01 and Archive 02 revelations if played chronologically. It must be placed later, converted into a retrospective investigation, or rewritten to reveal only partial contradictions.

# **ACT III — THE RETURN**

## **MISSION 07 — CONTACT LOST**

**Gameplay:** Reconnaissance, platform defense, rescue, extraction.

**Location:** Elyran ocean infrastructure.

**Premise:** A remote research platform loses contact after detecting unusual underwater movement.

**Objectives:**

- Reach the platform.

- Restore its sensors.

- Rescue surviving personnel.

- Defend against hostile forces.

- Escape when the platform is struck from below.

**Story:** Two enormous lights appear beneath the ocean.

The structure experiences impacts from something of extraordinary size.

**Updated lore connection:** This is a major Drowned Watcher encounter.

**Ending:** The team escapes without identifying the creature.

## **MISSION 08 — THE OTHER SIDE**

**Gameplay:** Slipscape travel, space reconnaissance, dogfight, wreck investigation.

**Location:** Route toward Astraea.

**Premise:** Human forces follow evidence connected to the Pathfinder and the Heralds.

**Objectives:**

- Escort the fleet through Slipscape.

- Investigate an unfamiliar signal.

- Defend against hostile fighters.

- Recover navigation and sensor data.

- Locate the route toward Astraea.

**Story:** Human forces begin realizing the conflict extends beyond the Empty Host.

**Updated lore connection:** The Codex and Pathfinder artifact may point toward routes deliberately excluded from ordinary navigation systems.

**Ending:** The fleet confirms that Astraea contains major ancient installations.

## **MISSION 09 — THE SECOND GARDEN**

**Gameplay:** Planetary reconnaissance, ground exploration, squad combat.

**Location:** Astraea.

**Premise:** Human forces encounter the ruins of the Herald civilization.

**Objectives:**

1.  Establish a landing zone.

2.  Investigate a Herald settlement.

3.  Recover surviving records.

4.  Identify competing Herald factions.

5.  Escape an escalating conflict.

**Story:** The Heralds are not a united invading force.

Their civilization has fractured.

**Updated lore connection:** The Hollow's influence helped intensify the conflict, but the player should not yet receive a complete explanation.

**Ending:** Michael discovers that Astraea's catastrophe resembles humanity's own history.

# **ACT IV — THE TRUTH BENEATH ASTRAEA**

## **MISSION 10 — THE EMPTY HOST**

**Gameplay:** Tactical assault, infiltration, data recovery.

**Location:** Empty Host military installation.

**Premise:** Michael attacks an installation used to coordinate the Hollow King's operations.

**Objectives:**

- Infiltrate the perimeter.

- Disable security systems.

- Fight elite Empty Host units.

- Recover research records.

- Escape the installation.

**Story:** The records reveal that the King has been studying ancient sites and attempting to control the Drowned Watcher.

**Updated lore connection:** The King may be attempting to suppress the Hollow's influence through increasingly authoritarian methods.

**Ending:** Michael discovers that the King knows more about the Great Collapse than the Keepers have admitted.

## **MISSION 11 — THE TESTAMENT**

**Gameplay:** Ancient exploration, historical reconstruction, puzzles.

**Location:** Restricted archive or ancient installation.

**Premise:** Michael discovers that the Codex is connected to the Testament of the First Word.

**Objectives:**

1.  Locate the surviving records.

2.  Restore the archive interface.

3.  Reconstruct fragmented historical events.

4.  Identify ancient routes.

5.  Recover evidence concerning the First Civilization.

**Story:** The records indicate that an ancient civilization developed a method of resisting the Hollow.

**Updated lore connection:** This is where the Pathfinder's discovery can become meaningful to Michael's main storyline.

**Ending:** Michael learns that the Hollow's destructive cycle may be resisted without destroying the force itself.

## **MISSION 12 — THE KING BENEATH THE STARS**

**Gameplay:** Infiltration, elite combat, character confrontation.

**Location:** Ancient command site associated with the Hollow King.

**Premise:** Michael confronts the King and demands an explanation for his campaign.

**Objectives:**

Enter the command site.

Fight through the King's defenses.

Recover strategic data.

Confront the King.

Escape the installation.

**Major revelation:** The King explains the Hollow's balancing function and its influence on advanced civilizations.

He argues that humanity cannot be trusted with increasing power.

Michael recognizes that the King's historical understanding may be accurate, but refuses to accept extermination as the only solution.

**Ending:** The King survives the encounter and allows Michael to leave, believing he may eventually understand.

# **ACT V — THE LAST KEEPER**

## **MISSION 13 — THE MOUNTAIN**

**Gameplay:** Cosmic horror, exploration, ancient machinery.

**Location:** Ancient Mountain complex on Astraea.

**Premise:** Michael and Elias enter an enormous ancient installation connected to the First Civilization.

**Objectives:**

1.  Enter the lower chambers.

2.  Restore ancient systems.

3.  Protect Elias during the investigation.

4.  Use Zeal to interact with machinery.

5.  Reach a sealed threshold.

6.  Escape the destabilizing complex.

**Story:** The structure contains impossible spatial geometry.

Its machinery appears connected to the First Civilization's method of resistance.

**Updated lore connection:** The sealed threshold must not imply the Hollow is simply a physical room or dimension containing a monster.

**Ending:** Michael discovers that the Spear may be capable of interacting with the ancient system in a different way.

## **MISSION 14 — THE SPEAR OF DESTINY**

**Gameplay:** Interceptor combat, orbital defense, ground assault.

**Location:** Spear-related military and ancient installations.

**Premise:** The conflict reaches a critical point as military commanders prepare to use overwhelming force.

**Objectives:**

Defend evacuation routes.

Protect allied ships.

Reach the Spear-related installation.

Survive the collapsing battlefield.

Confront the Hollow King.

**Story:** The King recognizes that indiscriminate destruction may worsen the catastrophe.

The older campaign proposed that he provides Michael with knowledge necessary to use the Spear before dying.

**Updated direction:** His final act may be a genuine attempt to preserve creation, but it must not erase his crimes.

**Continuity warning:** The older mission places this sequence on Astraea, while the built Archive 03 places the Spear climax at Tide City on Elyra. The revised full campaign must preserve the existing Tide City event unless that playable mission is deliberately redesigned.

## **MISSION 15 — THE FIRST WORD**

**Gameplay:** Final flight, evacuation defense, ancient technology, major choice.

**Location:** Final crisis zone, to be reconciled with Tide City.

**Premise:** Humanity faces a catastrophe that conventional military victory cannot resolve.

**Objectives:**

Protect surviving forces.

Prevent unnecessary destruction.

Reach the final ancient interface.

Apply the knowledge recovered from the First Civilization.

Make the final decision concerning the Spear.

**Main thematic choice:** Michael refuses the idea that preservation requires extermination.

**Proposed alternate resolution:** The Spear is used through a controlled interface that maintains universal balance while preventing immediate catastrophe.

**Established playable outcome:** Archive 03 currently depicts the Spear firing at Tide City.

**Secret-ending direction:** Preventing the Spear's charge while preserving the King's flagship and the main Herald ship may allow the player to discover another solution.

**Final image:** The immediate battle ends, but ancient machinery registers a distant response.

Something much larger has been affected.

## **5. RECOVERED FOOTAGE**

Three recovered-footage reels exist in the current material.

They should be treated as mysterious records whose presentation may not perfectly correspond to ordinary historical chronology.

Important established rules:

ELYRA never transported Michael's team to the Hollow King's mountain.

The Drowned Watcher has been warning Michael against using the Spear.

The physical creature in the dark is not the Hollow itself.

The third reel belongs after Archive 02 and before Tide City.

Michael's inscription dated March 16 is deliberate.

The secret main-menu sequence "The Call" should remain a mystery, not an exposition scene.

## **6. REVELATION ORDER**

The campaign should reveal information in stages.

**Early:** The Keepers' history is incomplete.

**Pathfinder S1:** The Empty Host possesses technology it did not create.

**Pathfinder S2:** The Heralds are divided and fighting one another.

**Pathfinder S3:** An older civilization discovered a method of resisting a catastrophic influence.

**Archive 01:** The Pathfinder crew is connected to the King's mountain.

**Archive 02:** The Heralds are refugees from a catastrophe.

**Later campaign:** The Hollow is a necessary universal counterforce.

**Final act:** Humanity may be able to resist the destructive cycle without destroying universal balance.

The player may know more than Michael through Pathfinder, but the complete nature of the Hollow should remain concealed until the intended main-campaign revelation.

## **7. CAMPAIGN PRODUCTION RULES**

- Space combat must remain a major gameplay pillar.

- Ground missions must have varied objectives.

- Horror should change player behavior rather than rely only on jump scares.

- The Hollow must not be represented as a conventional physical boss.

- The Heralds must not all be treated as enemies.

- The Hollow King's arguments must contain genuine truths.

- Michael must retain moral agency.

- The First Civilization's knowledge must not provide an effortless cure.

- The Watcher should remain rare and unpredictable.

- The older 15-mission concepts must be reconciled with the existing Archives before their dates and numbering are finalized.
