March 5: Hollow King takes the Codex. March 8: Pathfinder S1 raids the Empty Host installation on Astraea’s dead moon, steals the suppressor, and crashes on Astraea after the Pathfinder breaks apart in orbit. March 9: Pathfinder S2, Dead Ground, is planned on Astraea. March 10: Pathfinder S3, White City, is planned; evidence of ancient resistance, damaged transmission, capture of crew and suppressor. March 10–11: prisoners taken to the Hollow King’s mountain on Elyra; Watcher refuses to consume them. March 11–13: existing Archive 01. ## TIMELINE CONFLICT FLAGS

The older March 8 timeline says Michael dreams of Astraea’s white city; superseded by dreams of the Watcher and other significant figures, without implying prior Astraea travel. The older March 14 entry says Elias restricts access to evidence; reinterpret or revise that scene so he is not knowingly concealing the truth. Exact March 11–15 escape and transfer must be checked against Michael’s Archive 01 presence at the mountain.

## APPROVED UNIVERSAL RULES

The Hollow is a real, necessary, nonphysical universal balancing force. It cannot be killed or eliminated; its destructive influence can be resisted, managed, and greatly suppressed. It is not the Drowned Watcher or a conventional physical boss. The Hollow King recognizes the danger of the Hollow but is fully consumed by its influence. He understands suppression technology and rejects its implications. His fate in the existing game is defeat, not a confirmed death.

## SUPPRESSION TECHNOLOGY

The First Civilization invented the Hollow suppressor; the Heralds recovered and adapted it. Pathfinder steals a suppressor from the Empty Host moon installation on March 8, 2237. The device can project a protective dome of variable size, up to roughly a city (not a country). It requires no external power supply and massively reduces Hollow influence rather than granting immunity or destroying the Hollow. Working explanation, not yet a precisely defined mechanism: its protective strength may be sustained by the people inside actively resisting corruption, and may falter when that resistance collapses.

## HERALDS AND TIDE CITY

The Heralds were originally peaceful and possessed valuable knowledge, including the ancient suppression technology. Hollow corruption and a civil war fractured them. Their attack on Tide City is explained by the combination of civil war, Hollow influence, and misunderstanding; this is the agreed narrative direction, with detailed causes and participants still to be dramatized. Add more distinct Herald ships in the final battle, including ships that can function as combat distractions without implying every Herald is hostile.

## CHARACTERS AND REVELATIONS

Elias Veyr is a genuinely good, ordinary senior Keeper and mentor. He does not secretly possess the truth about Earth or the Hollow, does not intentionally conceal it from Michael, and has no supernatural powers. The Hollow King alone knows the deeper truth about Earth and the Hollow; surviving public histories or records do not automatically convey that full truth. Michael has foreshadowing dreams caused by a combination of Hollow influence and the Drowned Watcher’s mind-to-mind communication, plausibly aided by Elyra’s extensive water. Dreams should feature the Watcher and significant figures, not imply Michael visited Astraea, and should lead toward revelations rather than deliver explanations. Some dreams can become cinematic cutscenes.

## ENDING RULES

In the existing destructive Tide City route, the Spear kills everyone in the atmosphere/area affected by its discharge; Tide City is physically saved but humanity suffers a profound moral failure. Do not silently weaken this rule. The secret true ending is not literal killing of the Hollow: it is resisting/suppressing its corruption, avoiding slaughter of the Heralds, and making coexistence and possible peace achievable. Exact gameplay triggers and the reach of the Spear require alignment with the playable ending; do not replace them with invented conditions.

## PRESERVED MYSTERIES

Michael’s name appears carved beneath the Hollow King’s mountain with the date March 16, 2237. This is intentional and remains unexplained. The Pathfinder crew’s inscription bears March 8, 2237. The Drowned Watcher’s independence, the three recovered-footage reels, the ELYRA AI’s statement that she did not transport Michael’s team to the mountain, the menu secret “The Call,” and the phrase “Second Collapse” remain mysteries or unresolved continuity elements, not licenses to invent explanations.

## PATHFINDER-TO-KEEPERS TRANSFER

Pathfinder S3 ends March 10 with the Empty Host capturing the surviving crew and retaking the suppressor. Between March 11 and March 15, a survivor escapes or is freed from the Hollow King’s mountain, obtains the suppressor, and delivers it to the Keepers. This is an agreed continuity bridge; the survivor’s identity, means of escape, device recovery, and precise delivery scene/date remain open pending Pathfinder S3 and Archive cutscenes.

## PRODUCTION / CAMPAIGN RULE

Existing playable content has priority over the older speculative 15-mission outline: the prologue and Archives 01–03, Pathfinder S1, and existing footage/endings are not to be silently replaced. The goal remains a flexible 15-main-mission campaign, separate from Pathfinder’s three-mission side arc; earlier missions should have their own smaller Archives and escalating revelations. Next priority is Pathfinder S2. No new mission numbering or additional playable levels are approved by this update.

## **1. THE AGE BEFORE RECORDED HISTORY**

### **Era I — The First Civilization**

**Date: Unknown, potentially thousands or millions of years before the campaign.**

An unidentified civilization develops technology far beyond the capabilities of modern humanity and the Heralds.

Its influence extends across multiple worlds.

Its achievements include seamless white architecture, advanced energy systems, extraordinary spatial engineering, and machinery capable of interacting with consciousness.

The civilization eventually encounters the Hollow's destructive influence.

Unlike many civilizations, it discovers a method of resistance.

Evidence suggests it survives at least one period of severe Hollow influence without completely collapsing.

The ultimate fate of the civilization remains unknown.

### **Era II — The Abandoned Structures**

At an unknown point, installations associated with the First Civilization become abandoned or inaccessible.

Some remain on Astraea.

Others may exist on Elyra.

Their functions are no longer understood by later civilizations.

Their presence establishes that the history of intelligent life extends far beyond the records maintained by humanity.

## **2. THE AGE OF THE HERALDS**

### **The Rise of Astraea**

The Heralds develop an advanced civilization on Astraea.

They achieve interplanetary and eventually interstellar capabilities.

They discover ancient installations predating their own civilization.

Some attempt to study and preserve the technology.

Others seek to exploit it.

### **The Herald Division**

As the Herald civilization grows increasingly powerful, the Hollow's influence intensifies.

Political, military, scientific, and religious disagreements escalate.

Herald society fractures into competing factions.

Some believe ancient technology offers salvation.

Others believe the technology is responsible for the catastrophe.

Others attempt to escape.

The resulting conflict devastates parts of Astraea.

### **The Flight Toward Earth**

Herald survivors travel beyond Astraea.

They eventually encounter humanity.

Their arrival is remembered in human history as the beginning of the First Contact War.

Their warning is preserved in fragments:

"Something is coming."

## **3. THE GREAT COLLAPSE**

### **2154 — The First Contact War**

Humanity enters a major conflict involving the Heralds.

The full causes of the war remain disputed.

Humanity develops the Spear of Destiny using human and captured Herald technology.

The Spear is activated.

Its use contributes to damage within an ancient containment system.

Earth becomes uninhabitable or otherwise impossible to preserve.

The exact sequence of military victory, containment failure, and planetary destruction remains incompletely documented.

### **2154 — The Departure**

Nine evacuation arks flee Earth.

They carry surviving populations, technology, historical archives, religious traditions, and the foundations of humanity's future military institutions.

Only three eventually reach Elyra.

The fate of the other six arks remains an opportunity for future storytelling.

### **2157 — Arrival on Elyra**

**Proposed date, not yet locked.**

Three surviving arks reach Elyra.

The refugees discover an oceanic world containing unfamiliar native life and ancient white structures.

The survivors begin establishing permanent settlements.

## **4. THE REBUILDING OF HUMANITY**

### **2157–2170 — The First Settlements**

Humanity establishes coastal infrastructure and begins adapting to Elyra.

The white stone city becomes an important geographical and symbolic location.

Tide City develops nearby.

Surviving military organizations reorganize around the need to protect the remaining population.

### **2170–2200 — The Formation of Modern Institutions**

The Keepers develop into a combined military and religious authority.

The Remnant's beliefs become increasingly organized.

The Spear is preserved as a symbol of humanity's survival.

The Archive becomes the principal repository of historical and navigational knowledge.

### **2200–2230 — Expansion and Secrecy**

Humanity restores or expands its interstellar capabilities.

The Keepers maintain control over sensitive routes and technologies.

Some historical records become restricted.

The official understanding of the Great Collapse increasingly emphasizes military victory and survival.

The contradictions within the surviving records remain unresolved.

### **Before 2237 — The Hollow King's Campaign**

The Hollow King develops his understanding of the universal cycle.

He gathers forces into the Empty Host.

His army targets ancient installations and technologies.

He becomes convinced that humanity's advancement threatens creation.

His activities increasingly bring him into conflict with the Keepers.

## **5. THE MARCH 2237 CAMPAIGN**

### **March 5 — The Theft**

The Hollow King takes the Codex from the Keeper Archive.

The Codex contains essential navigational and historical knowledge.

The Pathfinder is dispatched in pursuit.

The King intends to control access to dangerous routes and technologies.

His complete motivation is not yet known to the player.

### **March 8 — Pathfinder S1**

Captain Rhen's team assaults an Empty Host installation on Astraea's dead moon.

The team recovers a mysterious white stone object.

Empty Host warships attack the Pathfinder.

The ship is broken into two sections in orbit.

Rhen's dropship escapes the destruction but crashes on Astraea.

Michael experiences a dream involving a white city beneath a red sky.

The date 08.03.2237 later appears beside the Pathfinder crew's names beneath the Hollow King's mountain.

### **March 9 — Pathfinder S2: Dead Ground**

**Planned mission.**

The surviving Pathfinder team crosses a devastated desert region of Astraea.

They encounter Herald factions fighting one another.

They scavenge supplies and locate the Pathfinder's second dropship.

They discover that both Herald factions are interested in the ancient object.

The team identifies a distant white structure.

### **March 10 — Pathfinder S3: The White City**

**Planned mission.**

Rhen's team enters an ancient structure predating the Heralds.

They discover evidence that another civilization resisted the Hollow's destructive cycle.

They attempt to transmit their findings to Tide City.

Rhen records a warning about the Heralds and the danger of repeating the Great Collapse.

The transmission is corrupted.

The Empty Host captures the team and the object.

Only a short, damaged portion of the transmission reaches humanity.

### **March 10–11 — The Prisoners**

The captured Pathfinder crew is taken to the Hollow King's stronghold on Elyra.

The King attempts to feed the prisoners to the Drowned Watcher.

The creature refuses.

The prisoners are confined beneath the mountain.

Their names appear carved into the cave walls.

The inscription bears the date of the original artifact theft, March 8.

### **March 11 — Archive 01: The Mountain**

Michael investigates the Hollow King's mountain.

He discovers the Pathfinder crew's names and hears Rhen's voice.

He encounters evidence of the Watcher and the Codex.

The Watcher takes the Codex into the underground lake.

The mystery surrounding the Pathfinder deepens.

### **March 13 — Archive 01 Ending**

Additional fragments of Rhen's message become available.

The recording reveals that the Heralds are not invading humanity.

They are running.

The human fleet prepares to travel to Astraea.

### **March 14 — Archive 02: The Heralds**

The fleet reaches Astraea.

The Pathfinder's two broken sections remain in orbit.

Its flight recorder is recovered.

Human forces encounter the surviving Heralds and discover evidence of their ongoing conflict.

The Covenant is destroyed.

The Pathfinder's recorder continues transmitting after being switched off.

Elias restricts access to the evidence.

### **March 14–15 — The Return**

The recovered-footage sequence involving the Watcher's warning belongs after Archive 02 and before Tide City.

ELYRA states that Michael's team was not transported to the Hollow by a vessel she commanded.

The Watcher's warnings increasingly focus on the Spear.

### **March 16 — Archive 03: The Empty Sky / Tide City**

The Hollow King's fleet attacks.

The conflict reaches Tide City.

The Spear fires in the established main route.

Michael's name is inscribed beneath the mountain with this same date.

The consequences of the Spear's activation establish the possibility of a Second Collapse.

### **Alternate March 16 Outcome**

**Future secret ending.**

If the player stops the Spear's charge and preserves both the Hollow King's flagship and the main Herald ship, an alternate resolution becomes possible.

This ending should connect to the First Civilization's method of resistance.

The complete mechanics and consequences remain to be designed.
