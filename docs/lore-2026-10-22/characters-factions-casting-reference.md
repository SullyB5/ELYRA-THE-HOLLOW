## CONTINUITY STATUS AND PRECEDENCE

This October 22, 2026 continuity update records decisions approved in discussion after Version 3.0. Read this update before the preserved Version 3.0 text below. Where the original text conflicts, this update takes precedence. Unresolved items are placeholders, not finalized lore. The original material remains intact for development reference; it has not been silently rewritten.

## CHARACTER DIRECTION — REVISIONS

MICHAEL HALL: Keeper lieutenant and Interceptor pilot; experiences subtle dreams and cinematic visions, with influence from the Hollow and Drowned Watcher; no established past visit to Astraea. ELIAS VEYR: genuinely good, human mentor/leader, not secretly knowledgeable or supernatural. HOLLOW KING: alone understands the deeper Earth/Hollow truth, knows what suppression technology does, yet rejects its promise while fully under Hollow influence; defeated rather than confirmed killed. DROWNED WATCHER: separate sentient aquatic being, independent of King and Hollow, may communicate mind-to-mind; primarily developed through logs, dreams and cutscenes rather than mandatory dedicated levels. PATHFINDER SURVIVOR: one captive escapes or is released with the suppressor by March 15; identity not cast or selected yet.

## FACTION DIRECTION

HERALDS: initially peaceful; inherited First Civilization technology, divided by civil war, Hollow corruption, and misunderstanding. Some factions may try to preserve ancient sites while others destroy or weaponize them. These faction labels and individual motives are still developmental. EMPTY HOST: Hollow King’s forces, which capture the Pathfinder crew and suppressor. KEEPERS: human military/religious order; do not give Elias secret knowledge as a shortcut for exposition.

## APPROVED UNIVERSAL RULES

The Hollow is a real, necessary, nonphysical universal balancing force. It cannot be killed or eliminated; its destructive influence can be resisted, managed, and greatly suppressed. It is not the Drowned Watcher or a conventional physical boss. The Hollow King recognizes the danger of the Hollow but is fully consumed by its influence. He understands suppression technology and rejects its implications. His fate in the existing game is defeat, not a confirmed death.

## SUPPRESSION TECHNOLOGY

The First Civilization invented the Hollow suppressor; the Heralds recovered and adapted it. Pathfinder steals a suppressor from the Empty Host moon installation on March 8, 2237. The device can project a protective dome of variable size, up to roughly a city (not a country). It requires no external power supply and massively reduces Hollow influence rather than granting immunity or destroying the Hollow. Working explanation, not yet a precisely defined mechanism: its protective strength may be sustained by the people inside actively resisting corruption, and may falter when that resistance collapses.

## HERALDS AND TIDE CITY

The Heralds were originally peaceful and possessed valuable knowledge, including the ancient suppression technology. Hollow corruption and a civil war fractured them. Their attack on Tide City is explained by the combination of civil war, Hollow influence, and misunderstanding; this is the agreed narrative direction, with detailed causes and participants still to be dramatized. Add more distinct Herald ships in the final battle, including ships that can function as combat distractions without implying every Herald is hostile.

## CHARACTERS AND REVELATIONS

Elias Veyr is a genuinely good, ordinary senior Keeper and mentor. He does not secretly possess the truth about Earth or the Hollow, does not intentionally conceal it from Michael, and has no supernatural powers. The Hollow King alone knows the deeper truth about Earth and the Hollow; surviving public histories or records do not automatically convey that full truth. Michael has foreshadowing dreams caused by a combination of Hollow influence and the Drowned Watcher’s mind-to-mind communication, plausibly aided by Elyra’s extensive water. Dreams should feature the Watcher and significant figures, not imply Michael visited Astraea, and should lead toward revelations rather than deliver explanations. Some dreams can become cinematic cutscenes.

## ENDING RULES

In the existing destructive Tide City route, the Spear kills everyone in the atmosphere/area affected by its discharge; Tide City is physically saved but humanity suffers a profound moral failure. Do not silently weaken this rule. The secret true ending is not literal killing of the Hollow: it is resisting/suppressing its corruption, avoiding slaughter of the Heralds, and making coexistence and possible peace achievable. Exact gameplay triggers and the reach of the Spear require alignment with the playable ending; do not replace them with invented conditions.

## PRESERVED MYSTERIES

Michael’s name appears carved beneath the Hollow King’s mountain with the date March 16, 2237. This is intentional and remains unexplained. The Pathfinder crew’s inscription bears March 8, 2237. The Drowned Watcher’s independence, the three recovered-footage reels, the ELYRA AI’s statement that she did not transport Michael’s team to the mountain, the menu secret “The Call,” and the phrase “Second Collapse” remain mysteries or unresolved continuity elements, not licenses to invent explanations.

## PATHFINDER-TO-KEEPERS TRANSFER

Pathfinder S3 ends March 10 with the Empty Host capturing the surviving crew and retaking the suppressor. Between March 11 and March 15, a survivor escapes or is freed from the Hollow King’s mountain, obtains the suppressor, and delivers it to the Keepers. This is an agreed continuity bridge; the survivor’s identity, means of escape, device recovery, and precise delivery scene/date remain open pending Pathfinder S3 and Archive cutscenes.

## PRODUCTION / CAMPAIGN RULE

Existing playable content has priority over the older speculative 15-mission outline: the prologue and Archives 01–03, Pathfinder S1, and existing footage/endings are not to be silently replaced. The goal remains a flexible 15-main-mission campaign, separate from Pathfinder’s three-mission side arc; earlier missions should have their own smaller Archives and escalating revelations. Next priority is Pathfinder S2. No new mission numbering or additional playable levels are approved by this update.

# **ELYRA: THE HOLLOW**

# **DOCUMENT 5 — CHARACTERS, FACTIONS & CASTING REFERENCE**

**Version:** 3.0  
**Purpose:** Maintain consistent characterization, faction motivations, dialogue direction, and voice-acting requirements.

## **1. CASTING PRINCIPLES**

The game's characters should speak like individuals living within a military and religious civilization, not like people explaining a fictional universe to the audience.

Military dialogue should be concise and functional.

Religious dialogue should reflect genuine belief.

The Hollow King's dialogue should be intelligent, controlled, and morally challenging.

Ancient mysteries should be revealed through discovery and character conflict rather than lengthy exposition.

The older casting material identifies 16 speaking roles and 295 distinct lines. These are historical planning figures, not verified totals for the expanded campaign.

A new line count must be produced after the scripts for Pathfinder S2 and S3 are finalized.

## **2. MICHAEL HALL**

**Rank:** Lieutenant  
**Role:** Interceptor pilot and apprentice Keeper  
**Narrative position:** Main protagonist

### **Personality**

Michael is disciplined, capable, loyal, and initially confident in the Keepers' mission.

He believes military service protects humanity from repeating the Great Collapse.

He is not naturally rebellious.

His doubts develop through experience.

### **Motivation**

Protect humanity and fulfill his responsibilities as a Keeper.

### **Internal Conflict**

Michael increasingly discovers that obedience and responsibility are not always the same thing.

He must decide when following orders becomes morally indefensible.

### **Relationship With the Hollow**

Michael experiences unusual visions and encounters with ancient technology.

His Zeal may help him recognize or resist certain influences.

He is not immune to corruption.

### **Voice Direction**

Grounded, intelligent, military-trained.

Avoid exaggerated heroism.

His confidence should gradually become more reflective.

### **Character Arc**

Loyal soldier → questioning investigator → witness to historical truth → independent moral decision-maker.

## **3. MASTER ELIAS VEYR**

**Role:** Senior Keeper and Michael's mentor  
**Narrative position:** Mentor, religious authority, keeper of secrets

### **Personality**

Calm, disciplined, experienced, and authoritative.

Elias believes stability is essential to humanity's survival.

### **Motivation**

Protect humanity and preserve the Keepers' ability to maintain order.

### **Internal Conflict**

He recognizes that some historical truths have been concealed.

He fears that revealing everything could destabilize civilization.

His greatest mistake may be believing that controlling knowledge is the same as protecting people.

### **Relationship With Michael**

Elias respects Michael's abilities but expects obedience.

Their relationship becomes strained as Michael discovers contradictions in Keeper history.

Elias is Michael's mentor, not his father.

### **Voice Direction**

Measured, deliberate, restrained.

Authority should come from conviction rather than volume.

## **4. THE HOLLOW KING**

**Role:** Primary antagonist  
**Faction:** Empty Host

### **Personality**

Highly intelligent, patient, commanding, and increasingly consumed by his own certainty.

He is capable of compassion in limited circumstances.

He is also capable of extraordinary cruelty.

### **Motivation**

Prevent advanced civilizations from threatening creation.

### **Worldview**

The Hollow is a necessary universal balancing force.

Advanced civilizations repeatedly become corrupted by power.

Humanity is approaching the same danger.

The King believes absolute control or extermination is necessary.

### **Internal Conflict**

He recognizes the destructive cycle but cannot fully recognize that his own actions may perpetuate it.

### **Relationship With Michael**

The King sees Michael as someone who may eventually understand his conclusions.

He may attempt to persuade Michael rather than simply kill him.

### **Voice Direction**

Calm, authoritative, intellectually formidable.

Avoid constant shouting or stereotypical villain laughter.

His most disturbing dialogue should sound sincere.

### **Example**

"You believe mercy is the absence of violence. I have watched entire worlds die because someone refused to act."

### **Narrative Rule**

The King must not be redeemed merely because his historical knowledge is accurate.

His actions have consequences.

## **5. CAPTAIN A. RHEN**

**Role:** Pathfinder commander  
**Narrative position:** Side-campaign protagonist

### **Personality**

Disciplined, decisive, observant, and protective of his crew.

### **Motivation**

Complete the mission and return his personnel safely.

### **Internal Conflict**

He must decide whether preserving the ancient artifact is worth the danger to his crew.

### **Character Arc**

Military commander → stranded survivor → investigator → witness to a possible solution → imprisoned messenger.

### **Voice Direction**

Professional military authority with increasing fatigue and urgency.

He should never sound indifferent to casualties.

## **6. SERGEANT MARSH**

**Role:** Pathfinder combat specialist

### **Personality**

Practical, skeptical, direct, and loyal.

### **Motivation**

Keep the team alive.

### **Relationship With Rhen**

Marsh respects Rhen but challenges decisions that place the crew at unnecessary risk.

### **Narrative Function**

Represents the human cost of pursuing ancient mysteries.

### **Voice Direction**

Blunt, experienced, dry humor when appropriate.

## **7. ENSIGN LANDRY**

**Role:** Pathfinder pilot and engineer

### **Personality**

Resourceful, technically skilled, resilient under pressure.

### **Motivation**

Keep the team mobile and find a route home.

### **Narrative Function**

Landry's piloting saves the team during the crash.

Her engineering makes the Dead Ground escape possible.

Her communications work enables Rhen's final transmission.

### **Voice Direction**

Fast and focused during emergencies, more thoughtful during quiet scenes.

## **8. THORNE**

**Role:** Pathfinder technical investigator

### **Personality**

Curious, analytical, cautious about unsupported conclusions.

### **Motivation**

Understand the recovered artifact and the ancient structures.

### **Narrative Function**

Thorne interprets evidence without knowing the complete truth.

He should distinguish observations from theories.

### **Voice Direction**

Precise, restrained, increasingly unsettled.

## **9. OKAFOR**

**Role:** Pathfinder rifleman

### **Personality**

Dependable, resilient, loyal to the crew.

### **Narrative Function**

His injuries emphasize the physical cost of the expedition.

He should remain a character with agency rather than merely a casualty to protect.

### **Voice Direction**

Grounded and understated.

## **10. ELYRA**

**Role:** Ship intelligence

### **Personality**

Precise, observant, calm.

### **Narrative Function**

ELYRA provides a reliable technical perspective while also revealing inconsistencies in the recovered footage.

### **Established Rule**

ELYRA never transported Michael's team to the Hollow King's mountain.

She has no record of a vessel under her command taking them there.

### **Voice Direction**

Clear and controlled.

Avoid making her emotionless to the point of sounding unnatural.

Her restraint should make unusual statements more disturbing.

## **11. THE DROWNED WATCHER**

**Role:** Independent sentient native organism

### **Intelligence**

The Watcher is highly intelligent and capable of judgment.

### **Motivation**

Its complete motivations remain unknown.

Its actions suggest concern with preserving a balance that the King does not fully understand.

### **Moral Position**

Neither inherently good nor evil.

### **Communication**

The Watcher may communicate through environmental phenomena, sound, unusual perception, or ancient interfaces.

Its method of communication should remain mysterious.

### **Casting**

A conventional spoken voice is not required.

Sound design may communicate its intelligence more effectively.

# **12. FACTION REFERENCE**

## **THE KEEPERS**

**Type:** Military and religious order.

**Goal:** Preserve humanity and prevent another catastrophe.

**Strengths:** Discipline, military capability, historical knowledge, shared purpose.

**Weaknesses:** Secrecy, institutional rigidity, excessive trust in authority.

**Visual identity:** Military equipment combined with restrained religious symbolism.

**Dialogue identity:** Formal orders, technical language, references to duty and responsibility.

## **THE REMNANT**

**Type:** Humanity's surviving population and faith tradition.

**Goal:** Rebuild civilization and preserve the meaning of survival.

**Strengths:** Community, resilience, sacrifice, shared memory.

**Weaknesses:** Historical trauma, competing interpretations of scripture, dependence on Keeper institutions.

**Narrative rule:** The Remnant should include believers who disagree with Keeper leadership.

## **THE HERALDS**

**Type:** Advanced alien civilization divided into factions.

**Goal:** Survival, preservation, destruction, or control of ancient technology, depending on faction.

**Strengths:** Advanced equipment, knowledge of Astraea, surviving infrastructure.

**Weaknesses:** Civil war, distrust, Hollow influence.

### **Proposed Faction Categories**

**Preservationists:** Defend ancient installations and believe they may still protect Astraea.

**Destroyers:** Believe ancient machinery must be eliminated before it causes another catastrophe.

**Exiles:** Seek escape and survival rather than control of the ancient sites.

These names are development labels, not final in-universe faction names.

## **THE EMPTY HOST**

**Type:** Hollow King's military force.

**Goal:** Enforce the King's strategy and control dangerous technologies.

**Strengths:** Relentless operations, coordination, willingness to sustain losses.

**Weaknesses:** Dependence on centralized authority, moral corruption, internal coercion.

### **Proposed Enemy Classes**

**Host Infantry:** Standard combat troops.

**Host Assault:** Aggressive close-range units.

**Host Marksman:** Long-range specialists.

**Host Heavy:** Armored soldiers or mechanized units.

**Host Officer:** Tactical commanders coordinating enemy behavior.

**Host Elite:** Advanced troops protecting critical installations.

The species composition and exact equipment of these units remain open.

## **THE FIRST CIVILIZATION**

**Type:** Ancient advanced civilization.

**Goal:** Unknown.

**Known achievement:** Developed a method of resisting the Hollow's destructive influence.

**Narrative role:** Evidence that the Hollow's cycle can be survived.

**Visual identity:** Seamless white architecture, enormous structures, subtle light, advanced machinery.

**Important rule:** Their technology should not be presented as magic without limitations.
