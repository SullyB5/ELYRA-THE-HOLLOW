# First Game — Campaign & Mission Design Document

**Campaign format:** Linear, story-driven single-player

**Planned length:** 15 main missions across five acts

**Design goal:** Every mission must provide a clear objective, a distinct gameplay experience, and meaningful story progression.

1\. Campaign at a glance

| **Act**                        | **Missions** | **Narrative purpose**                                                     |
|--------------------------------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| I — The Known War              | 1–3          | Establish Michael, the Interceptors, Elias, and the first anomalies       |
| II — Something Is Wrong        | 4–6          | Reveal ancient machinery and show that the official history is incomplete |
| III — The Return               | 7–9          | Escalate the war and reveal Astraea as the Heralds' homeworld             |
| IV — The Truth Beneath Astraea | 10–12        | Expose humanity's mistake and confront the Hollow King's ideology         |
| V — The Last Keeper            | 13–15        | Reach the sealed threshold and use the Spear to make a different choice   |

**Intended progression:** Patrol → Investigation → Escalating Warfare → Historical Revelation → Cosmic Horror → Final Choice.

2\. ACT I — THE KNOWN WAR

# MISSION 01 — FIRST WATCH

**Gameplay:** Interceptor flight tutorial, scanning, dogfight, investigation

**Location:** Orbital corridor and oceanic approaches to Elyra

## Premise

Michael Hall begins a routine patrol near a human-controlled orbital corridor. A damaged transport sends a distress call and reports a systems failure. Its crew mentions something beneath the water before the transmission cuts off.

## Objectives

1.  Launch from the carrier and learn flight controls.

2.  Navigate to the damaged transport.

3.  Scan the vessel and attempt to establish contact.

4.  Engage hostile fighters when they attack.

5.  Recover the flight recorder before the wreckage is lost.

6.  Return to the carrier for debriefing.

## Story beats

- The crew is missing, and the damage does not clearly match the reported attack.

- The recorder contains a repeating signal unlike known human or Herald transmissions.

- During the investigation, sensors detect two enormous lights beneath Elyra's ocean. They appear to move, then vanish.

- The squad dismisses the lights as reflections or sensor error.

- The signal continues after the recorder is switched off.

## Mission ending

Command classifies the incident as an enemy attack and orders Michael to return. The unexplained signal is logged but not investigated.

**Purpose:** Teach flight combat and establish the first Watcher foreshadowing without revealing the creature.

# MISSION 02 — THE QUIET SETTLEMENT

**Gameplay:** Ground movement, scanning, squad combat, exploration, extraction

**Location:** Remote Elyran settlement

## Premise

A remote settlement has stopped responding. Michael accompanies a Keeper security team to investigate.

## Objectives

7.  Land at the settlement perimeter.

8.  Search homes, a medical station, and the communications facility.

9.  Restore power to the transmitter.

10. Collect evidence of the sudden disappearance.

11. Locate the source of a signal beneath the settlement.

12. Defend the extraction zone and withdraw.

## Story beats

- Meals remain on tables, equipment is still running, and no conventional battle is evident.

- A distress message ends mid-sentence.

- A teammate hears their own voice over a disconnected radio, repeating a sentence they have not said yet.

- Recovered data points to an ancient signal beneath the settlement.

## Mission ending

The squad finds a sealed access point under a utility structure. The ground vibrates, lights fail, and something moves beneath the site. The squad extracts before opening the access point.

**Purpose:** Establish the ground-combat loop and make environmental investigation central to the mystery.

# MISSION 03 — ORDERS FROM VEYR

**Gameplay:** Carrier hub, escort, dogfight, pursuit, debrief

**Location:** Human fleet near Elyra

## Premise

Master Elias Veyr takes control of the investigation and orders the recovered equipment transferred to Keeper custody.

## Objectives

13. Receive a briefing from Elias.

14. Escort a transport carrying the recovered technology.

15. Defend the transport against a coordinated fighter ambush.

16. Pursue a hostile craft through debris.

17. Return to the carrier and review the evidence.

## Story beats

- Enemy fighters target the recovered equipment rather than the easier military targets.

- Elias recognizes a symbol but calls it an obsolete religious marking.

- He orders Michael to keep the discovery out of the official report.

- Michael begins to distrust his commander's explanation.

## Mission ending

The transport survives, but the attackers escape with part of the data. Elias warns Michael that knowledge can be dangerous when people use it without understanding it.

**Purpose:** Establish Elias as a mentor with secrets and demonstrate how missions connect through a military hub.

3\. ACT II — SOMETHING IS WRONG

# MISSION 04 — THE BURIED SIGNAL

**Gameplay:** Ground combat, ancient exploration, machinery puzzles, defensive encounter

**Location:** Buried complex beneath the settlement

## Premise

The signal is traced to an underground complex older than known human civilization.

## Objectives

18. Enter the complex with a Keeper squad.

19. Restore power to a sealed chamber.

20. Protect technicians while they retrieve an artifact.

21. Investigate a corridor whose internal dimensions appear impossible.

22. Escape when the structure reacts to the team's presence.

## Story beats

- Ancient systems activate when Michael approaches.

- Zeal first becomes observable to the Keeper team.

- A sealed door opens onto a corridor longer than the structure could contain.

- An inscription translates to: \*\*“They came before the end.”\*\*

## Mission ending

The complex begins to destabilize. Elias orders the site sealed and recognizes the inscription without explaining why.

**Purpose:** Make ancient technology tangible and show that Michael can interact with it in an unusual way.

# MISSION 05 — THE EMPTY CHAPEL

**Gameplay:** Survival horror, exploration, limited combat, resource management

**Location:** Abandoned religious site on Elyra

## Premise

A Keeper expedition disappears at a chapel referenced in forbidden Remnant scripture.

## Objectives

23. Search the chapel and adjoining crypts.

24. Locate the missing expedition's equipment.

25. Restore emergency lighting and communications.

26. Collect the lead researcher's recording.

27. Reach extraction while an unknown presence moves through the dark.

## Story beats

- The chapel's symbols resemble Herald markings.

- Equipment suggests the expedition found something beneath the building.

- A researcher left a final recording: \*\*“The stories were never about angels coming to save us.”\*\*

## Gameplay direction

Ammunition and visibility are limited. Some threats should be avoided or escaped rather than fought. Horror should come from uncertainty and sound, not constant combat.

**Purpose:** Connect the Remnant's religious history to the ancient conflict and shift the player's behavior from attack to survival.

# MISSION 06 — THE FORBIDDEN RECORD

**Gameplay:** Stealth, archive investigation, security systems, short combat encounter

**Location:** Restricted Keeper archive

## Premise

Michael follows the chapel evidence into a restricted archive. Elias arranges access, but custodians have been ordered not to cooperate.

## Objectives

28. Access a restricted records terminal.

29. Reconstruct fragmented historical files.

30. Avoid or disable archive security.

31. Retrieve the surviving records.

32. Escape when Keeper command seals the facility.

## Story beats

- Records describe the Heralds as refugees fleeing an unknown catastrophe, not simply invaders.

- The official history of the Great Collapse is incomplete.

- Elias admits he has known part of the truth for years.

- An emergency alert interrupts his explanation.

## Reveal limit

Do not yet explain what the Heralds fled or exactly how humanity damaged the containment system.

**Purpose:** Establish institutional secrecy while preserving the campaign's major revelations for later acts.

4\. ACT III — THE RETURN

# MISSION 07 — CONTACT LOST

**Gameplay:** Large-scale space battle, Interceptor combat, defensive flying, retreat

**Location:** Elyra's orbital defense network

## Premise

An unidentified fleet arrives. Command declares an invasion and scrambles every available Interceptor squadron.

## Objectives

33. Launch under emergency conditions.

34. Protect defense platforms and civilian transports.

35. Engage enemy fighters and attack craft.

36. Respond to emergency calls from an oceanic monitoring station.

37. Withdraw when the formation is overwhelmed.

## Story beats

- The enemy uses technology resembling artifacts Michael recovered.

- Some ships bypass military targets to strike ancient structures.

- The oceanic station detects a massive object beneath the water.

- Two enormous lights reappear; a coastal platform is struck from below and disappears into the disturbed sea.

- Michael never gets a clear view of the creature's complete body.

- Command identifies the attackers as surviving Herald forces.

## Mission ending

Humanity loses control of the region despite winning a limited tactical engagement. The Watcher remains classified as an unexplained oceanic anomaly.

**Purpose:** Escalate the conflict and bring the Watcher back as a more concrete but still largely unseen threat.

# MISSION 08 — THE OTHER SIDE

**Gameplay:** Ground assault, tactical combat, machinery recovery, objective defense

**Location:** Captured installation connected to an ancient structure

## Premise

Michael's unit retakes an installation occupied by the invading force.

## Objectives

38. Advance through the outer defenses.

39. Secure the command center.

40. Investigate a machine believed to be an enemy weapon.

41. Protect technicians as the machine is examined.

42. Prevent both sides from destabilizing the system further.

43. Escape with a damaged data core.

## Story beats

- The machine is part of a containment system, not a conventional weapon.

- Enemy forces were attempting to prevent human activation.

- The fighting destabilizes the machinery.

- The enemy withdraws with a damaged data core rather than pursue a tactical victory.

## Mission ending

The evidence suggests the enemy was trying to prevent a disaster. Michael's command still calls it a retreat.

**Purpose:** Complicate the player's view of the enemy without minimizing the reality of the war.

# MISSION 09 — THE SECOND GARDEN

**Gameplay:** Atmospheric flight, landing, exploration, ancient ruins

**Location:** Astraea

## Premise

Michael joins an expedition to Astraea after recovered data links the planet to the ancient machinery.

## Objectives

44. Navigate hostile orbital debris.

45. Land near a ruined settlement.

46. Secure the landing zone for researchers.

47. Explore a vast abandoned city.

48. Recover a navigation record from an ancient structure.

## Story beats

- Astraea is revealed as the Heralds' original homeworld.

- The ruins show a civilization destroyed by a sudden catastrophe.

- An ancient structure activates when Michael approaches.

- A transmission uses voices associated with people lost during earlier missions.

- A message repeats: \*\*“Do not open what we could not close.”\*\*

## Mission ending

Michael finds evidence that the Heralds fled Astraea rather than planning to invade humanity.

**Purpose:** Deliver the first major historical revelation and identify Astraea as the center of the mystery.

5\. ACT IV — THE TRUTH BENEATH ASTRAEA

# MISSION 10 — THE EMPTY HOST

**Gameplay:** Combined-arms battle, Interceptor escort, ground assault, evacuation

**Location:** Human positions near Astraea's ancient sites, with a related oceanic operation on Elyra

## Premise

The Hollow King's forces attack human positions near ancient sites. Their targets appear carefully selected.

## Objectives

49. Escort friendly dropships in an Interceptor.

50. Destroy enemy emplacements supporting the assault.

51. Hold a defensive line while civilians and researchers evacuate.

52. Recover a fallen Keeper officer's encrypted orders.

53. Investigate evidence linking the Empty Host to the ancient systems.

## Story beats

- Empty Host forces isolate particular structures rather than destroying everything indiscriminately.

- Michael discovers that Keeper command knew the ancient sites were connected to containment.

- A classified report from an oceanic research facility on Elyra describes a massive creature striking the facility from below.

- The signal associated with the creature matches the pattern detected in earlier missions.

- Evidence suggests the Empty Host is using an ancient device to influence the Drowned Watcher's movements.

## Mission ending

Michael confronts Elias about the Keepers' role. Elias admits that some Keepers continued research despite warnings about the ancient systems.

**Purpose:** Reveal that the war's strategic goals are connected to the containment system and establish the Watcher's weaponization.

# MISSION 11 — THE TESTAMENT

**Gameplay:** Archive exploration, investigation, defensive combat

**Location:** The Testament of the First Word, Astraea

## Premise

Elias leads Michael to an archive buried beneath Astraea. “Codex” is merely the Keeper classification; the archive's true name is the Testament of the First Word.

## Objectives

54. Enter the archive before hostile forces arrive.

55. Restore damaged records and retrieve missing segments.

56. Defend Elias and the archive during a security breach.

57. Escape with the recovered testimony.

## Major revelation

- Humanity combined human and captured Herald technology to create the Spear of Destiny during the Great Collapse.

- Using the Spear interfered with a containment system connected to Astraea.

- Humanity did not create the Hollow, but it weakened the barrier.

- Returning to Astraea and activating ancient machinery repeated the same mistake.

## Character revelation

Elias admits that the Keepers preserved fragments of the truth but allowed history to become doctrine. He believed secrecy would protect humanity, but it left humanity unprepared.

## Mission ending

The Testament points to the Mountain and indicates that the Spear may reinforce the failing barrier if activated through Zeal.

**Purpose:** Deliver the central historical revelation and prepare the final act.

# MISSION 12 — THE KING BENEATH THE STARS

**Gameplay:** Infiltration, elite enemy combat, confrontation, escape

**Location:** Ancient command site on Astraea

## Premise

Michael infiltrates a command site occupied by the Hollow King to stop him from destroying the remaining containment infrastructure.

## Objectives

58. Infiltrate the command site.

59. Fight through elite Empty Host forces.

60. Retrieve the site's data core.

61. Confront the Hollow King.

62. Escape as the structure begins to fail.

## Major revelation

The Hollow King explains that the Heralds were fleeing the Hollow and that humanity's actions weakened the containment system. He believes humanity will repeat its mistakes and allow the Hollow to spread through creation.

He is correct about the danger but wrong to conclude that humanity must be exterminated.

## Drowned Watcher reveal

The King admits that he learned to influence the Drowned Watcher using ancient technology. He did not create it. The creature is native to Elyra, and his control is imperfect. He refuses to say whether the creature could become more dangerous if the signal were lost.

## Mission ending

Michael escapes without defeating the King. The King allows him to leave because he believes Michael may still understand the danger, even if they disagree about the answer.

**Purpose:** Make the ideological conflict explicit and connect the Watcher to the King's larger campaign.

6\. ACT V — THE LAST KEEPER

# MISSION 13 — THE MOUNTAIN

**Gameplay:** Ground exploration, environmental puzzles, survival horror, ancient machinery

**Location:** The Mountain, Astraea

## Premise

Michael and Elias descend into the Mountain, a colossal structure connected to the containment system.

## Objectives

63. Navigate the lower chambers.

64. Restore sections of the ancient mechanism.

65. Protect Elias while he interprets the system.

66. Reach the deepest accessible level before the structure destabilizes.

## Story beats

- Corridors seem larger inside than outside.

- Sound travels incorrectly and equipment registers spaces that should not exist.

- Ancient machines activate without recognizable power sources.

- Michael reaches a sealed threshold associated with the Hollow but does not enter it.

- Elias determines that the Spear can reinforce the barrier if Michael interfaces with it through Zeal.

## Mission ending

The Mountain destabilizes. Michael and Elias escape as ancient systems activate. The Hollow King's forces launch a final assault.

**Purpose:** Deliver the campaign's most intense cosmic-horror sequence without revealing the inside of the Hollow.

# MISSION 14 — THE SPEAR OF DESTINY

**Gameplay:** Interceptor combat, orbital defense, ground assault, evacuation, character climax

**Location:** Astraea's orbital defenses and the Spear complex

## Premise

The containment system begins to fail. Human command prepares a full-scale strike against the enemy fleet, believing that destroying it will end the crisis.

## Objectives

67. Keep the orbital corridor open.

68. Protect evacuation transports and support ground forces.

69. Reach the Spear complex.

70. Survive the collapse of the command site.

71. Confront the Hollow King for the final time.

## Story beats

- Michael's flight section combines dogfighting, defensive escort, and emergency navigation.

- On the ground, Michael secures the route to the Spear while protecting personnel stabilizing the system.

- The Hollow King arrives and recognizes that destruction alone cannot preserve the barrier.

- He gives Michael the knowledge required to use the Spear, then dies during the collapse.

- His final action is meaningful but does not erase his crimes.

## Mission ending

The orbital battle continues. Astraea's survival depends on Michael's decision.

**Purpose:** Unite the military campaign, the King's ideology, and the Spear's true purpose.

# MISSION 15 — THE FIRST WORD

**Gameplay:** Final flight, collapsing battlefield, ground climax, ancient technology, final defense

**Location:** Astraea, the Mountain, and the containment network

## Premise

Astraea is collapsing. Human command orders Michael to destroy the enemy fleet. The Spear offers another possibility: reinforce the containment barrier and save the people on the planet.

## Objectives

72. Fly through the collapsing orbital battlefield.

73. Protect the route for evacuation and ground operations.

74. Reach the core chamber.

75. Activate the Spear through Zeal.

76. Defend the activation process until the barrier forms.

## Final choice

Michael refuses to treat victory as synonymous with annihilation. Rather than using the Spear to destroy every enemy in reach, he uses it to reinforce the barrier and protect Astraea's people.

## Resolution

- The Hollow's immediate advance is stopped.

- Astraea survives, badly damaged.

- The human and Herald forces remain divided.

- The political and religious consequences are unresolved.

- No one can prove that the Hollow is gone forever.

## Final image

The battlefield falls quiet. Deep beneath the Mountain, something responds to the newly sealed barrier. A faint signal appears, different from the one detected in Mission 1. The structure seems to respond to something beyond the threshold, then goes silent.

**Purpose:** Resolve Michael's emotional arc and the immediate crisis while preserving the cosmic mystery for a future game.

7\. Drowned Watcher appearance plan

The Watcher should remain rare. Its impact comes from scale, uncertainty, and the fact that the player sees its effects before its full body.

| **Mission**                     | **Appearance**                                           | **What the player learns**                                                |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 — First Watch                 | Two lights under Elyra's ocean                           | Something is beneath the water, but it may be a sensor error              |
| 7 — Contact Lost                | Lights reappear; a coastal platform is struck from below | The anomaly has physical force and enormous scale                         |
| 10 — The Empty Host             | Research report and signal evidence                      | The Empty Host appears able to influence its movements                    |
| 12 — The King Beneath the Stars | Direct explanation from the King                         | The King directs the creature but did not create it; control is imperfect |
| Future installment              | Optional underwater mission                              | Severing the control signal may free the creature but carries risk        |

Do not force a full Watcher boss fight into the first game. If a playable encounter is added, the objective should focus on survival, evacuation, or disabling the control device—not necessarily killing the organism.

8\. Gameplay pillars and variety

# Space flight

Space flight is a core gameplay pillar, not a transition. Use patrols, dogfights, escorts, reconnaissance, station defense, pursuit, debris navigation, orbital defense, evacuation protection, and ground support. Not every flight mission should be a wave-based dogfight.

# Ground operations

Use tactical assaults, defensive holds, rescue, extraction, investigation, stealth, ancient exploration, machinery recovery, and protection of researchers. Give the player meaningful reasons to move between combat and exploration.

# Horror

Horror should change player behavior: conserve ammunition, restore power, follow unreliable signals, avoid unknown entities, protect squadmates, and retreat when necessary. Keep the military competent; the fear comes from confronting something outside its knowledge.

# Approximate balance

Aim for roughly six flight-focused missions, six ground-focused missions, and three combined missions. Treat this as a design target, not a rigid rule. The priority is distinctive rhythm and purpose.

9\. Revelation pacing

- \*\*Missions 1–3:\*\* Something is being hidden.

- \*\*Missions 4–6:\*\* The official history is false or incomplete.

- \*\*Missions 7–9:\*\* The enemy has a purpose beyond ordinary conquest; Astraea is the Herald homeworld.

- \*\*Missions 10–12:\*\* Humanity helped weaken the containment system; the Hollow King's ideology is revealed.

- \*\*Missions 13–15:\*\* Michael learns how the Spear can preserve rather than dominate and chooses differently.

10\. Character arcs

# Michael Hall

- Missions 1–3: The Lieutenant — trusts orders and discipline.

- Missions 4–6: The Investigator — learns obedience and truth can conflict.

- Missions 7–12: The Witness — sees humanity's responsibility and the enemy's motives.

- Missions 13–15: The Keeper — uses power without trying to dominate everything around him.

# Elias Veyr

Elias recognizes ancient symbols but withholds explanations; helps Michael access restricted records; confesses the Keepers' failures; accompanies Michael to the Testament and Mountain; and eventually trusts Michael to make a decision Elias could not make himself.

# The Hollow King

The King remains dangerous and accountable. He understands the threat but believes extermination is the only answer. His final act gives Michael necessary knowledge without absolving him.

11\. Environmental storytelling

Use visual evidence instead of explaining everything through long recordings:

- A settlement abandoned mid-activity.

- A transport recorder that continues transmitting after shutdown.

- A chapel whose symbols contradict official doctrine.

- A sealed archive with deliberately missing records.

- Enemy troops protecting or avoiding ancient machinery.

- A ruined Herald city on Astraea.

- Watcher lights beneath an otherwise calm ocean.

- A platform torn apart from below.

- The Mountain's impossible interior spaces.

- The Spear chamber where an old weapon becomes a tool of preservation.

12\. What the first game answers

The campaign should answer:

- Why the Heralds came to Elyra.

- What humanity did during the Great Collapse.

- Why the Hollow King believes humanity must be destroyed.

- Why the Empty Host targets ancient structures.

- Why the Spear can potentially reinforce the barrier.

- Why Michael's Zeal matters.

- Why the Drowned Watcher is being used as a weapon.

It should only partially answer:

- What first awakened the Hollow.

- Why Zeal works.

- What Elias knew and when he learned it.

- What happened to the Heralds who stayed on Astraea.

- How much the Hollow King truly understands about the Watcher.

It should leave for future stories:

- What lies beyond the Mountain's threshold.

- The ultimate origin of the Hollow.

- Whether the Hollow is a place, force, or intelligence.

- Whether the barrier will hold permanently.

- What happens to the Drowned Watcher if its control signal disappears.

13\. Narrative guardrails

77. Do not reveal the full nature of the Hollow in the opening missions.

78. Do not make the Drowned Watcher the source of the Hollow or a routine boss.

79. Do not present the Hollow King as wholly right or grant him an easy redemption.

80. Do not make the Keepers uniformly evil; their failure is rooted in secrecy, fear, and institutional loyalty.

81. Do not take Michael inside the Hollow in the first game.

82. Do not let the Spear solve every problem without cost, risk, or a meaningful choice.

83. Keep space combat central and ground missions varied.

84. Let discoveries arise from objectives, environments, and character decisions—not only exposition.
