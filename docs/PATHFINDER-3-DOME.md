# Pathfinder · Arc 03 · The White City — the dome (Bryce's idea, Oct 22)

**The idea:** at the end of Arc 03 the crew reach the middle of the white city, the place the object fits. It goes in, and a dome
rises over the whole city. They see it work, and then the Empty Host take them. This is what the mission finishes on.

## Does it fit the lore? Yes, almost word for word

From `docs/lore-2026-10-22/pathfinder-the-lost-expedition.md`:

- **Suppression technology:** the First Civilization made the suppressor; it projects a protective dome "up to roughly a city",
  needs no outside power, and *reduces* the Hollow's influence (no immunity, doesn't destroy it). Its strength may depend on the
  people inside resisting, and may falter when they can't.
- **S2 brief:** "subtle shots of the object to foreshadow its later city-scale application." Arc 02's calm-zone moment near the case
  is that foreshadowing. The White City dome is the payoff.
- **S3 premise:** "The artifact appears connected to its machinery." The city was built around the socket.
- **Rhen's message:** "There was another people here before them. They found a way to stop it." Now he's saying it *after seeing it*,
  which makes the line land. (Keep it meaning resist/suppress, as the continuity update asks.)
- **Ending as written:** "The white structure begins shutting down" when the officer carries the object away. That's the dome dying.

## The two rules it has to respect

1. **The object stays unnamed, and Rhen doesn't know what the Hollow is.** So go with the *"what is this? how does it work?"*
   version, not the crew understanding it's against the Hollow. The player understands (they've played the Mountain); the crew only
   half do. Thorne reads the effect, not the cause: the air goes still, the static on their radios clears, the hum they've been hearing
   since the moon stops. Something like:
   - LANDRY: "What is that?"
   - THORNE: "I don't know. But whatever's been in the air since we landed... it's gone."
   - RHEN: "They built this to keep something out."
2. **S3 still ends in capture.** The dome isn't a wall: it weakens the Hollow, it doesn't stop soldiers. Five exhausted people can't
   hold up a dome the size of a city, so it thins and flickers when the Host arrive, and they walk in through it. An officer pulls the
   object from the socket and the dome falls. (It also shows what the lore says about the Hollow King: he understands suppression
   technology, which is why he wanted it back.)

## The whole mission (Bryce, Oct 22, 17:09)

1. **Arrival:** the repaired dropship flies in through a great circular opening in the city's wall (or lands at its front door).
2. **Through the city:** fight street by street. Herald defenders, Herald attackers trying to destroy the place, and the first
   **Empty Host** units already down in the city: all three sides fighting each other and you. The Host showing up early is the
   warning that the capture is coming.
3. **The building:** the crew push toward one tower and go up it (or down into it), not knowing it holds the socket the object fits.
4. **The room:** fight in the chamber at its heart; seat the object.
5. **Dome cutscene,** Rhen's warning, the Host land, the dome **flickers and fails**, capture (below).

("Seeing the Hollow" here means its army, the Empty Host: per the lore the Hollow itself is never shown as a creature.)

## Order of the last stretch

Lore phases 5 to 7 with the dome in them:

1. **The heart of the city** (Phase 5, The Threshold): a round open plaza or chamber at the centre, a socket in a raised ring.
   Gameplay: hold the Herald attackers off while Thorne seats the object.
2. **The dome** (cutscene, ~20–30 s): the object sinks in, gold lines run out through the streets, the dome climbs from the city's
   edge and closes overhead. Seen from the plaza, then from outside the city. The crew's three lines above.
3. **The warning** (Phase 6): Rhen transmits from inside the dome. "We were wrong about the Heralds... They found a way to stop it."
4. **Capture** (Phase 7): Landry: "Something's entering the atmosphere." Host dropships come down outside the dome. The dome flickers.
   Fight you can't win, overwhelmed, restrained. The officer pulls the object; the dome falls in a wave from the top. Rhen sees the
   comm light still blinking. Cut to black.

## Decided (Bryce, Oct 22)

**City-sized dome, and it flickers when the Host arrive.** Showing it in the Pathfinder doesn't spoil anything later: the Pathfinder
missions are the lead-up to the ending of Last Transmission, so the player should know what the dome is going in. Tide City only gets
fragments of Rhen's message, so the Keepers still don't.
